HOOK‑A‑DUCK

Fairground duck pond

Move the pointer — you hold the rod by its grip.
Hold to lower the hook, release to reel it in.
Slide the wire through a hoop and lift.
Two ducks at once pays double.
Keep the rhythm and the streak multiplies.
The last 15 seconds are the final rush.
