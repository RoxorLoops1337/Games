HOOK‑A‑DUCK

Fairground duck pond

Move the pointer — you hold the rod by its grip,
and the hook hangs on the line ahead of it.
Hold the button (or Space) to lower the hook.
Release to reel it back up — if the hook slid
through a ring, the duck comes up with it.
The hook holds two ducks at a time — a pair pays double.
Every duck has a number stamped underneath, and keeping
the rhythm going builds a streak multiplier.
