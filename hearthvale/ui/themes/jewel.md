Glass Jewel (id `jewel`)
Technique: SVG lighting filters (feDiffuse/feSpecularLighting on a blurred alpha height map, feTurbulence caustics and refraction, feMorphology rim) on the ::before layer of small buttons, plus conic-gradient gem facets, chamfered clip-path crystal slabs and liquid-gem bars.
Touchable because: every button is a coloured translucent gem (ruby, emerald, sapphire, topaz, amethyst, rose quartz) with a dark rim, a bright glow pooled at the lower edge, a sharp specular pinpoint; the light follows the pointer or finger over a button (and the tilt of the phone), pressing sinks the gem, dims the body and flares the light under the finger.
Performance: filters only on buttons (about 10-30 small layers, static so they are cached; one shared live filter moves for the single hovered button, rAF throttled; tilt updates at most every 160 ms), backdrop blur on 3 surfaces (dock, quest tracker, banner), init takes about 5 ms, one 144 px canvas tile as data URI. `body.lowfx` drops all filters, blur and animation and keeps the gradient gems.
Weaknesses: dark violet slabs make the UI heavier than the pastel world; lighting differs slightly between browsers (Safari renders fePointLight coordinates less precisely, Firefox ignores filters on pseudo-elements in some versions, then the gradient gem remains); iOS tilt needs a permission we do not request.
