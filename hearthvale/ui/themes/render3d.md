Rendered 3D (id render3d): glossy PBR plastic buttons, plates, frames, rings and a level seal rendered by three.js.
Technique: render3d.js builds rounded plates (concentric inset contours, bevel plus dome profile, vertex-colour gradients) in a MeshPhysicalMaterial clearcoat studio (PMREM softboxes, key/fill/rim), orthographic sheared camera, soft shadow plane and a gloss decal pass; 67 PNG sprites become blob: URLs in --r3-* custom properties and CSS draws them as 9-slice border-image.
Feels touchable: a visible dark saturated under-edge, a real specular band and bounce light, a tight contact shadow, a second darker thinner pressed render plus translate, a finger-direction tilt, and stroked outline text.
Performance: no committed assets; the sprites render one by one in the background after selection (about 0.3 to 0.8 s on a GPU, 7 to 18 s in headless software GL), then the WebGL context is released; 96 root properties, about 1.1 MB of blob memory at 2x (more at dpr 3), no per-frame cost.
Fallback: if WebGL or toBlob fails (or while sprites render) a CSS-only candy look applies; families switch on one by one through html[data-r3]; dispose removes listeners, attributes, properties and revokes every blob URL.
Weaknesses: first paint shows the CSS look for a moment; sprite corners are fixed radius (22 pill, 20 plate) so very tall boxes read as rounded rects; tiny items use stretched dot sprites; blob sprites are soft at dpr 1 on huge buttons.
