Gilded Fantasy (id gold): royal violet velvet panels with gold frames, cast metal plates with enamel inlays, Genshin / Hearthstone menu feel.
Technique: runtime-generated inline SVG data URIs published as CSS custom properties by gold.js (9-slice rope and scallop frame edge, 4 filigree gem corners, folded ribbon, laurels, level medallion with wreath, ornamental rule, feTurbulence brushed-metal and velvet grain), plus conic/linear hard-stop metal gradients in gold.css.
Touchable because: every button is a thick plate (dark outline, bevelled rim in gold, silver or copper, engraved groove, glass-gloss enamel in purple, emerald, ruby or sapphire, embossed serif text), a slow glint sweeps the metal, and a press sinks the plate by its own thickness while the glint flashes.
Performance: assets built in under 10 ms and rasterised once as background images; the only motion is background-position glints on small plates (no blur animation); one drop-shadow filter each on the sheet ribbon and banners; body.lowfx removes glint, brushed grain and filters; reduced motion stops all glints.
Contrast: cream and gold text on velvet is 7:1 to 10:1, enamel button text is at least 4.5:1 (darkened enamels), large titles at least 3.5:1.
Weaknesses: filigree corners are fixed-size art (panel corner radius is 14 px, not the base 32), Georgia digits depend on the platform serif font, the ribbon stretches vertically on tall banners, and the brushed texture is subtle on small buttons.
