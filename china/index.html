我们的故事Our Story

我们是来自丹麦的 beatbox 和歌唱二人组，2017 年开始一起演出。一开始只是因为我们都爱音乐，后来一路唱到了大大小小的舞台、学校、音乐节和国际活动上。

我们想做的事很简单：用音乐跟大家连在一起。人声 beatbox，加上有力量的歌声和真实的能量。我们相信音乐能把人聚在一起。

We are a Denmark-based beatbox and singing duo, performing together since 2017. What started as a shared passion for music has grown into a journey across stages, schools, festivals and international events.

Our goal is simple: to connect with people through music, combining human beatboxing, powerful vocals and genuine energy. We believe in the power of music to bring people together.
