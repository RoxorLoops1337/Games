Our story

两个人，一个声音Two voices, one sound

我们是一对来自丹麦的 Beatbox 与歌唱二人组。从共同的音乐热爱出发，我们一路走上学校、音乐节和国际舞台。

我们的目标很简单：用音乐与人相连。人声 Beatbox、有力的歌声，加上真诚的能量，把大家聚在一起，带来灵感与难忘的回忆。

We are a Denmark-based beatbox and singing duo. What started as a shared passion for music has grown into a journey across stages, schools, festivals and international events.

Our goal is simple: to connect with people through music, combining human beatboxing, powerful vocals and genuine energy.
