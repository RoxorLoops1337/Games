# Recorded meows: sources and licences

`meows.wav` holds 27 meows cut from the recordings below (silence and room noise trimmed, long files split into single meows,
faded, level matched, packed together). `tools/make_bank.py` rebuilds it, `meow_bank.js` is generated by that script.
The raw files are not in the repo. Everything else in the game's audio (effects, the old synth meows, all music) is made in code.

| Clips | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| b1 to b15 | `ANMLCat_Cat_meow_made_with_mouth_ID_0926`, BigSoundBank.com | CC0 (BigSoundBank publishes its sounds as public domain) |
| m1, m2 (two meows in one file), s1 | Mixkit "cartoon kitty begging meow" and "sweet kitty meow" | Mixkit Sound Effects Free License: free to use in a project, no attribution needed, but the files may not be redistributed on their own |
| k1 | `kitten_mew-1.ogg` | origin not known, please confirm before shipping widely |
| a1, g1, g2, c1 to c4, h1 | generated by the project owner with an AI sound tool | the terms of that tool apply |

To drop a clip, delete its source from the folder or its entry in `SOURCES` in `tools/make_bank.py` and run the script again.
