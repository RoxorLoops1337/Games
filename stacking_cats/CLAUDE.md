# Stacking Cats

Live at `https://games-71g.pages.dev/stacking_cats/`. Plain ES modules, no build step; `build.js` only copies the folder.

- `cats.js`: data only (32 cats, snacks, 61 relics, 9 Kingdom buildings and their bonus rules, enemy types, themes, unlock rules, meta-stat helpers). Add a cat by adding an entry here AND a draw block in `art_cats.js` (`ACC[id]`). `tests/stacking_cats_sim.test.mjs` checks every cat attacks and has text.
- Relics (the `ITEMS` in `cats.js`, called items in the code, relics in the game): 61 of them in five rarities `RARITIES` = common, rare, epic, legendary, mythic (23, 18, 11, 6, 3). Odds per drop are `ITEM_WEIGHT` per relic times the number of relics of that rarity, and luck (Kingdom, route, Fortune Cookie, Golden Paw, the Cat King's crown) lifts the higher tiers; three of a tier forge into the next (`FORGE_NEXT`, three mythics pay coins), `SELL_VALUE` and the shop's `itemPrice` cover all five. A relic is `{ id, name, icon (a unique emoji used as the key of its drawing), rarity, fx, desc }` and `fx` may only use keys in `FX_KEYS` (exported from `sim.js`, the comment above it explains each: per cat damage-type effects, conditional damage `exec`/`open`/`bossDmg`/`pristine`, kill effects `kheal`/`kcoin`/`kcharge`/`boom`, `cleave`, `venom`, `frenzy`, barrier `shield`, `undying`, `echo`, `spc`, and team auras `teamDmg`/`teamHaste`/`teamHp`/`teamDr`/`curseT`/`wheal`/`zoomG`/`luck` that count while a living tower cat carries them). The hooks are in `hurtEnemy` (conditional damage, cleave, venom), `killEnemy` (kill effects, with a depth cap on chain explosions), `hurtCat` (barrier, undying), `calcMods` (auras), the cat loop in `step` (frenzy, spc, echo) and `startWave` (barriers and per wave counters reset). Drawings live in `art_relics.js` (merged into `art_icons.js` before `ICON_KEYS` is read, a test checks that); a new relic needs its drawing, an entry in the tests' counts, and a mechanic test in `tests/stacking_cats_extra.test.mjs`. Rarity colours are in `index.html` (`.r-legendary`, `.r-mythic`, `.bag.legendary`).
- Difficulty: `TUNE` (`hpA`, `hpB`, `dmgA`, `dmgB`) sets how mice grow per wave. `tests/stacking_cats_balance.test.mjs` has two yardsticks: the plain bot (never uses specials, plays far worse than a person) and an attentive bot (`skill: 1`, fires specials and zoomies). The goal is that a fresh save with the attentive bot ends around wave 15 and only about one run in ten reaches the wave 30 finale; change `TUNE` and read the printed spreads.
- `sim.js`: the whole game rules, pure and seeded (`newGame({seed})`, `step(g, dt)`). No DOM. The renderer reads state and drains `g.ev` for visual and sound events. Tuning knobs are in `TUNE`.
- `art_util.js`, `art_cats.js`, `art_world.js`, `art_kingdom.js`: all art is procedural canvas drawing (no image files). Cats are drawn once into a 120x120 box and cached by `main.js`.
- `post.js`: lighting and post stack (multiply light map, thresholded bloom, colour grade, grain, flare) and `ambientFor` (day to dusk to night colour). Themes in `cats.js` carry `amb`, `grade`, `weather`, `haze`. `main.js` has `gfx` levels 0-3 (off, light, weather, bloom) that the adaptive quality drops before it lowers resolution; Low quality skips the whole stack. Far and mid backdrop layers get depth of field blur and haze in `art_world.js`.
- `art_icons.js`: every icon is drawn (no emoji shown). Data files keep an emoji as the icon id; `installGlyphs` swaps known emoji in the DOM for drawn `<img class="gi">` and `iconCanvas(key)` serves canvas code. A new icon character needs a drawing in `D` (a test fails otherwise). Loot: the backpack holds `MAX_STASH` but a pick is never refused (it may overflow, and the arrange screen blocks Done until the player sells, forges 3 of a rarity into 1 of the next, or equips). Sell and forge live in `sim.js` (`sellItem`, `forgeItems`).
- Canvas memory: phones cap total canvas memory and then blank sprites and backdrops (a player saw an empty night scene with only HUD and rain). Only the current theme's backdrop is kept (`setBackdrop` frees the rest with `freeBackdrop`), temp canvases are zeroed after use, and caches are rebuilt when the tab becomes visible again. Keep new per-theme full-size canvases to a minimum.
- Fight agency (`sim.js` end block, data in `cats.js` SPECIALS/EVENTS): every cat has a special move (`useSpecial`, charged by time and kills, tray under the HUD, keys 1 to 6, tap a charged cat) and tapping a mouse sets `g.focus` (cats shoot it first). Event stops (`rollEvent`/`chooseEvent`, shop, shrine, rest, deal, training) come every third non-nest wave through the reward queue. New mice: dasher (leaps onto the top cat), shield bearer (shield blocks status effects), digger (untargetable while `under`). Each nest has a trick (`NESTS[].trick`, in `nestAI` and `hurtEnemy`). Assist mode: Settings toggle or the A key (`save.opt.autoSpecial`, off by default, an AUTO badge shows over the tray) fires charged specials by itself; the rules are the pure `autoSpecialPick(g)` in `sim.js`. Keys: Space zoomies, 1-6 specials in a fight (or the nth card on a pick screen), Enter presses the open screen's main button, Escape presses its Back, arrows move through cards (and through the Kingdom buildings and wonders, Enter buys), A auto, F speed, P pause, M sound, D meter, G fullscreen. Tests: `tests/stacking_cats_fight.test.mjs`.
- Install and sharing: `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` (every game module must be in its PRECACHE list, a test checks it; network first for code, cache first for fonts and icons; bump its version constant when you change cached file names), `pwa.js`, `haptics.js`, `share.js` (share card). Music: `audio.js` has 18 songs (6 themes by calm, fight, boss) played by a look-ahead scheduler; it was never listened to by the author, so tune by ear.
- Recorded meows: `meows.<hash>.wav` (27 meows, mono 24 kHz, about 1.2 MB, in the `sw.js` PRECACHE) is fetched and decoded after the first tap (`Sfx.loadMeows`/`setMeowBank`); until then, offline or if it fails, everything uses the synth voices. `meow_bank.js` is generated by `tools/make_bank.py` from the raw recordings (not in the repo, see `AUDIO_CREDITS.md`): never edit it by hand. The bank file name carries a content hash (a test checks it): after running the script put the new name in the `sw.js` PRECACHE and bump `VERSION`. `meow_voice.js` is pure: `meowPlan(catId, mood)` picks a clip and pitch per cat and mood, `clipForNote(freq)` the recording that sings a note with the least shifting, `MEW_KIT` the meow drums. In `audio.js`, `sampleAt` plays a slice of a clip; songs with `mew: N` have every Nth lead note (and long final notes) sung by a meow (`mewlead`, falls back to the song's own lead), songs with `mewKit: true` use meow kick, snare and hat (the kick keeps a synth thump underneath). Six songs use it (calm Garden Morning, Cheese Waltz, Petal Drift; fight Garden Rush, Mouse Hole Mambo, Hanami Charge). Loudness was matched to the old synth versions by rendering offline in Chromium (within about 1.3 dB); redo that if you change `MEW_LEAD_GAIN` or the kit. Settings has a Cat meows toggle (`opt.meows`, `sfx.meowsOn`).
- Meta: mastery (`g.mastery`, +2% damage and +1% health per level above 1), cosmetics (`cosmetics.js`, `save.owned`, `save.wardrobe`), Nightmare ladder (`DIFFS` nm1..nm5, `save.ladder`), weekly challenge (`weekKey`, same Pages Function with `?type=weekly`).
- `main.js`: renderer, effects, HUD and all DOM screens. `audio.js`: synth sound, cat voices and a step-sequenced soundtrack (calm, fight, boss).
- `art_util.js` also holds small pure helpers with tests in `tests/stacking_cats_pwa.test.mjs` (`idleHop`, `waveProgress`, `dangerLevel`, `clearLine`, `confirmTap`); `daily.js` has `passedBest`, `milestoneLine`, `bestTag`, `dailyStreak`. Keep UI numbers and wording that can be tested as pure functions there. `tests/stacking_cats_extra.test.mjs` ends with a seeded fuzz of whole runs (random specials and picks, all difficulties) that fails on NaN values or throws.
- Story: `story.js` (pure: the Whiskerton plot, intro scene timeline and captions, rescue and game over lines with cat puns) and `intro.js` (the anime style cutscene drawn live on one 960x540 canvas from the game's own sprites; `drawIntro(ctx, t, R)` is a pure function of time, `playIntro` adds skip controls and sound cues). It plays once before the first run (`save.seenIntro`), from How to play and from the title Story button. Chapters, taunts, freed cat lines, per cat stories and the finale (wave 30, the last nest of the first trip around the map: `showFinale`, once per run, +5 gems once ever via `save.storyDone`, then the run goes on) live in `story.js` as pure helpers; the finale card has a Watch the ending button that plays `playEpilogue` (two scenes in `intro.js`, same runner as the intro). Both files are in the `sw.js` PRECACHE. Keep captions free of em dashes.
- Small pure modules with their own tests: `kingdom_scene.js` (the tappable village), `savecode.js` (save export/import, cleans imported data), `daily.js` (daily score and the leaderboard client, never throws).
- `../functions/api/stackingcats_board.js`: daily leaderboard (Cloudflare Pages Function, KV binding `BOARD`, 503 without it so the game hides the board). KV TTL must stay >= 60.
- Save key `stacking_cats_save_v1` (never rename). Unlock stats and coins are committed when a run ends; `save.kingdom` holds coins and building levels (additive fields only).
- Progression: coins earned in a run go to the Cat Kingdom; building levels become `g.meta` (see `kingdomBonuses`) and are added in `calcMods`. Items live on `cat.items` (cached sums via `itemFx`), the backpack is `g.stash`. `g.curCat` marks which cat is acting so damage, kills and healing are credited (damage meter, wave report, game over chart). Synergies come from `CAT_TAGS` and `SYNERGIES` (counted over living tower cats, applied in `calcMods` and `cfx`). Routes, difficulty and the daily modifier are multipliers on `g.route`, `g.diff` and `g.daily`. The daily challenge ignores Kingdom bonuses and uses a fixed cat pool. Gems (second currency) buy wonders. `estDps` feeds the before/after previews, so keep it in step when you change attack numbers.

Tests: `npm run test:stackingcats` (sim rules, kingdom/items/coins/stats, a bot that plays whole runs with and without a built kingdom to keep difficulty fair and progression meaningful, and a headless art and wiring smoke test).
When you change `TUNE`, enemy stats or cat numbers, run the balance test and read the printed wave spread.
No em dashes in any text.
