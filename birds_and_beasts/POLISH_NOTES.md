# POLISH NOTES — The Birds & The Beasts

Running critique log for the polish loop. Two critics play the build, grade it and
issue demands; build agents work through them; the critics re-grade from scratch.
Whatever survives becomes the next round's brief.

## THE GAME, in one paragraph

An **autobattler** about breeding monsters, in a **9:16 portrait** frame.
NEST (two beasts in to breed, pick a ritual, optionally take a hazard) → FIELD
(prep: scout their team, tap beasts into an *ordered* line of 4, spend a Feed
budget; then commit and the match resolves itself while you watch) → CLUTCH
(keep one chick, sell the rest) → CULL (the barn holds 10). You never act during
a match. The front of each line takes every blow. Losing costs health, not the
run.

Hard rules the loop must never break: one self-contained file, exactly one
`<script>`, no external resources, no renamed element ids, no change to the
`window.BB` surface, and **no balance or rules changes** — `statPower`,
`deriveCost`'s sqrt curve, `ENEMY_BASE`/`ENEMY_GROWTH`/`GRUNT_FRAC`,
`TRAMPLE_CAP`, `LINE_SLOTS`, `LINE_BASE`/`LINE_GROW`, `MAX_DECK`,
`breed`/`geneRoll`, rituals, hazards and trait effects are simulation-tuned.
**Breakpoints are `@container app (...)`, never `@media`** — `#app` is the
container, and a viewport query will not fire correctly inside it.

size is readable; the replay no longer spoils its own outcome (cosmetic `vhp`

mid-word in a sideways scroller with two thirds of the choice off-screen (they
wrap into a grid now); the verdict printed on top of the mid-line's own words;

# OPEN ITEMS

## Visual design — 6/10
> Real progress: the flyer is anchored, the vw units are gone, the field finally has a floor, a horizon, dust and a forecast bar, there is a verdict, and `vform` gave the painter an actual silhouette vocabulary. It no longer looks broken. But it still does not look designed. Five of eight screens are a widget stack floating in 200-330px of black nothing — the Cull has a 240px canyon between the barn and its buttons, the Title plate uses 55% of a 9:16 phone, the Wild offer wraps 2+1 and lights no button at all. The barn grid still lands 4+4+2 on desktop and the exact 3+3+3+1 I named last round on mobile. And the match — the thing this genre is sold on — is two static rows 450px apart with "THEY MEET" between them, which is a caption for an event that never happens. Fix the voids, make the lines collide, and give the verdict a body, and this is an 8.

## Game feel — 6/10
> Eleven of my twelve demands landed, and several landed well — the replay no longer spoils itself, the lag ghost makes a blow's size readable, the killing blow freezes the frame, the verdict slams, the trample is a genuine wound, the forecast exists, the clutch holds its breath before the last egg. The game has a spine now. What it still does not have is a PAYOFF: you win a match and the game gives you nothing you can see — the heal, the +2 ceiling and the Slop are all applied silently off-screen while a lime headline says "3 STILL STANDING", so the reward loop of an autobattler is empty at the exact moment it should close. Below that sit three smaller rots: tapping a beast into your line doesn't commit (the card vanishes, a tile materialises, thud), tapping one you can't afford does literally nothing (sfxDeny and .card.nope are both written and never called), and the pink duel thread is drawn straight through the forecast sentence it is supposed to support. Fix the payoff and the touch response and this is a 8; today it is a well-built machine that forgets to hand you anything.
