How it works
You breed monsters for a living; your barn is your herd. The two in the Nest sit the match out, so you field a short line — that is the price of a clutch. On the field you choose who goes out and in what order, and pay Feed for them; then the match resolves itself and you watch. The front of each line takes every blow: Might is what it hits for, Hide is how long it stands there.
What gets past the top floor eats an egg — the round still ends, and the pair in the Nest still breeds. Every clutch throws two chicks (three from a Fertile parent) and you keep one. The rest go to market for Slop. Breed two relatives and the gene pool gets… interesting.
