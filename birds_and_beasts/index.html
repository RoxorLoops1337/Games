How it works
You breed monsters for a living; your barn is your herd. The two in the Nest sit the match out, so you go into the pit a pair short — that is the price of a clutch. Then the gates open. They come in from the right in waves, a beast at a time, and you send yours out to meet them by tapping a card — whenever you can feed it. Feed comes back on its own clock, so the whole fight is a question of what to spend it on and when. Might is what a beast hits for, Hide is how long it stands there. Beasts of yours that go down limp back to the barn and can be sent out again.
Anything that reaches the nest takes a bite out of your eggs — the round still ends, and the pair in the Nest still breeds. Every clutch throws two chicks (three from a Fertile parent) and you keep one. The rest go to market for Slop. Breed two relatives and the gene pool gets… interesting.
