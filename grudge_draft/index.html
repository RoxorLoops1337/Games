How it works

The idea

Two households have had enough of each other. Nobody throws the first punch, so instead you both draft people, line them up in the cul-de-sac and let it happen.

A round

  • You are offered three people. Tap one. That happens three times, so you keep three per round.
  • Panic rerolls the current three. Twice a match, no more.
  • Hit let them at it and the fight runs itself. You do not control anyone. Nobody would listen anyway.
  • The side left standing wins the round. The other side loses a nerve. Lose three nerves and it is over.

It stacks up

Everybody comes back next round, bruised but present, and you draft three more on top. By round four the cul-de-sac is a warzone of nans and traffic wardens. That is the intended experience.

Losing helps a bit

Drop a round and you get a pity pick, one extra choice next round. Being behind is survivable.

Cliques

People with the same tag get on. Two officials bury the other side in paperwork, three family members egg each other on, two exes know exactly where it hurts. Watch the bar above your squad, it lights up when a clique is live.

Tips

  • A wall of health with nothing behind it loses. So does five wasps.
  • Big slow hitters need someone to soak for them. Nan is happy to.
  • Two of a tag beats three unrelated strangers, most of the time.
