Four hundred tractors. One angry countryside. One officer with a baton, a belt of tear gas and a serious opinion about queue-jumping. Go out there and teach them a lesson… politely.

HOW TO PATROL

MOVE
WASD / arrows — walk the tarmac
up & down steps into the crowd
BATON

TEAR GAS
K or X — lob a canister
the cloud makes a whole crowd cough
SHOULDER CHARGE
L or C — barge through
bowls over everyone in the lane
CUFF THEM
I or V — arrest a dizzy farmer
big points, fills the siren
CALL BACKUP
SPACE when the siren is full
the whole shift arrives at once
