# GRAFTWORK

"Cut what you love. Stitch what you need." A roguelike deckbuilder where you never add cards: after a fight you pick ONE of your OWN cards and ONE graft to sew onto it. Every graft has a Boon and a Price (cost up, exhaust, self damage, jam, junk, slower draw, max HP): never free. Live at `https://games-71g.pages.dev/graftwork/`. Plain ES modules, no build step. Art is drawn on canvas and audio is synthesized, except the Ward art, the room kit and the two previews (painted UI parts, character rigs): see Art pipelines. **No em dashes or en dashes in any text, ever** (`hygiene` enforces it on code, tests, tools and docs).

## The one rule that makes it a game
- Each graft has a **Weight** (1 to 3). Deck **Budget** is 6 (+ relics, + Forge "Expand", + skipping a graft). Total weight over Budget = **Strain**: each Strain point lands one **Tithe** on a *Bare* card (no grafts, not junk), the cheapest, then oldest. Tithed cards fight at -25% numbers. The UI shows who will be tithed BEFORE you commit.
- Strain with no Bare card left is **Backfire**: every 2 overflow, one grafted card in your opening hand each turn is jammed (+1 cost).
- Max 2 grafts per card. The **3rd graft is an Overload**: +1 weight, damage/block x1.5, and on its first play each combat a 25% **Shatter** (the card resolves, then is destroyed for the run).
- **Temper** (Forge): fuses a card's patches into it (`inst.fused`, apart from `inst.grafts`): boons AND prices stay, slots empty, no Overload, each fused graft weighs 1 less (min 1), the card is not Bare, fused grafts never move, at most `FUSE_CAP` (4) (`canTemper`, `temper`, `fusedWeight`).
- Offers: 3 of your cards (one always a low-play-count card) x 3 grafts, one per tier: **safe** (weight 1), **greedy** (2 to 3, big boon), **weird** (changes how the card works). Skipping: heal 8, OR +1 Budget for good, OR +10 gold.
- **The Thread** (design/THREAD.md): cards of one school extend the Line, cutting it cashes the school's payout (3 paying cuts a turn, Line length 5).

## Card schema
`{ id, name, hero, type: 'attack'|'skill'|'power'|'junk', cost: 0..3|'X', rarity: 'starter'|'common'|'uncommon'|'rare'|'junk', target: 'enemy'|'all'|'self'|'none', school?, flavor (one dry line, max 60 chars), flags: {exhaust, innate, retain, ethereal, unplayable}, fx: [ops], up: { fx: [ops] (full replacement), cost?, unflags?: [] } }`. Powers leave the deck for the combat once played. Every non-junk card needs `up.fx` and `flavor`. Each hero needs >= 20 non-starter cards (`data` checks it).

## Ops (fx arrays). `n` is a number OR `{ base, per: <source>, mul, max, min }` (base + floor(source * mul))
- `{op:'dmg', n, hits?, to?:'enemy'|'all'|'random', pierce?}` (an enemy's hits the player); dmg and block scale with pct, Overload and Tithe.
- `{op:'block', n, to?}` `{op:'status', s, n, to?:'enemy'|'all'|'random'|'self'|'player'|'allies'}` `{op:'setStatus', s, n}` `{op:'double', s, cap?, to?}` `{op:'detonate', s, mul?}` `{op:'cleanse'}`
- `{op:'draw', n}` (max 4) `{op:'energy', n}` (max 3) `{op:'heal', n}` `{op:'hp', n}` (lose HP, never kills the player) `{op:'maxhp', n}` `{op:'gold', n}` `{op:'addCard', card, n, to:'hand'|'draw'|'drawTop'|'discard', up?}` `{op:'summon', id, n?}` (enemies)
- `{op:'discard', n, choose?}` `{op:'exhaustHand', n|'all', choose?, draw?}` `{op:'fetch', n, from:'discard'|'exhaust'}` `{op:'if', cond, ifText, then:[ops], else?:[ops]}` (cond `{src, lt|lte|gt|gte|eq}` or `(c, ctx) => bool`) `{op:'repeat', n, fx}`
- `{op:'custom', fn(A, ctx), text}` is the escape hatch: ALWAYS give `text`. Any op may carry `t:'override text'`. "Cut the Line." is `{ op:'custom', fn: (A) => A.cutLine(), text:'Cut the Line.', seam:'cut' }` (`seam:'cut'` is what `seamPreview` reads).
- Sources for `per` / `cond.src`: x hand energy block hp hp% missingHp drawPile discard exhaust plays turn deck grafts cardGrafts strain enemies kills dealt gold junk attacksInHand, `st:<status>@self|target|player`.
- Replays (Plays twice, Echo) repeat damage, block and status, NOT resource ops (draw, energy, gold, addCard, heal). Caps: plays a turn 20, hand 10, status 99, lifesteal 6 per play.

## Graft schema
`{ id, name, school, weight: 1|2|3, tier, rarity: 'common'|'uncommon'|'rare', boon, price, mods: [...], types?: ['attack'..], not?: [graftIds], ok?(spec) }`. `mods` holds at least one boon mod and one price mod (engine/cards.js `applyMod`):
- boon: `{k:'cost', d}` `{k:'flag', f}` `{k:'unflag', f}` `{k:'repeat', n}` `{k:'addFx', fx, pos?:'start'}` `{k:'pct', pct}` `{k:'aoe'}` `{k:'lifesteal', cap?}` `{k:'target', t}` `{k:'type', t}` `{k:'custom', hooks}`
- price: `{k:'selfHurt', n}` `{k:'jam', p}` `{k:'junk', card, n, to?, when?:'combatStart'|'onPlay'}` `{k:'selfStatus', s, n}` `{k:'drawdown', n}` `{k:'goldCost', n}` `{k:'maxHp', n}` (once, when grafted), plus `cost +1`, `flag exhaust`, `flag ethereal`, `pct -N`.
- `custom` hooks `{ onPlay(A, ctx), onDraw(A, {card, spec}), onDiscard, onExhaust, onTurnStart }` (weird grafts live here; ctx = `{card, spec, target, mult, x, replay, dealt}`). Thread flags on a hooks object: `noCut` (a would-be cut goes Slack), `wild` (extends any Line, adds no count).
- A Price has ONE definition, `isPriceMod`. Free Sample waives exactly those mods (`spec.waived`; print a Price on a card through `priceOnCard(spec, gid)`, which says Waived), Numb keeps exactly those. A price written as a hook is its OWN custom mod with `price: true`. A waiver belongs to one sewing: graft join and leave a card only through `sewGraft` / `unsewGraft`; move a graft with `R.moveGraft` (strip then add charges the Price again: `e_back_alley_swap` still does that, an open owner decision).
- Points: boon about +4 / +6 / +8 by rarity, net 0 / +1 / +2 after the price (cost +1 is -4, exhaust -3). Schools: ember echo hoard blood rust static gilded.

## Enemy, status, relic and event schemas
- Enemy (see `data/enemies_1.js`): Act 1 normal 38 to 44 HP and 7 to 9 damage a turn, x1.6 Act 2, x2.4 Act 3, elite x1.8 HP, bosses 130 / 220 / 340. Moves are declarative `fx` (hit the player by default; `to:'self'|'allies'` for buffs); the intent icon comes from the fx, `hidden:true` hides it. `pick(A,e)->moveId` or `pattern` / `opening`; `e.data` is free scratch; `art: { body, color, ... }` is read by art/enemies.js; statuses can be `immune: [ids]`.
- Enemy hooks `on: { combatStart(A,e), turnStart, damaged(A,e,dmg,{src,hit}), death, playerCard(A,e,{card,spec,target}), afterMove(A,e,moveId), modDamage(A,e,n,o)->n, statusGained(A,e,id,n), playerBlock(A,e,n), seamExtend, seamCut, seamCash, seamPay }`. `A` = combat API: `A.dmg(src,tgt,n,o)` block status setStatus heal loseHp draw energy gold addCard summon kill `A.cutLine()` ev rng player alive() `A.c`.
- Status: `{id, name, good, stack:'int'|'dur', canNeg?, mod:{outAdd,outMul,inMul,inCap,blockAdd,blockMul}, hooks:{turnStart,turnEnd,attacked,damaged,cardPlayed,kill}, desc(n)}`; hooks get `(A, owner, n, payload)`. A status definition stays within 170 characters, any other keyword within 120 (`tip_lifecycle`).
- Relic: `{ id, name, rarity, text, mod?: {energy, hand, budget, shatter, keepBlock, seamCap, resLens}, on?: { combatStart(A,p,run), turnStart, turnEnd, cardPlayed(A,{card,spec,target}), damaged, kill, exhaust, shatter, combatEnd, wouldDie, seamExtend, seamCut, seamPay } }`; run-level effects are `perk: 'name'`, read by engine/run.js.
- Event (`data/events.js`): `{ id:'e_x', name, act: 0|1|2|3 (0 any), text (2 to 4 short sentences), choices: [{ label, text?, cond?(run), pick?: 'card', pickFilter?(inst), apply(run, R, inst?) -> 'result' }] }`, at least 2 choices, one always safe. Persistent state in `run.flags`; change the run only through `R`: gold heal hurt (never kills) maxHp addCard addBuild removeCard upgrade addRelic addPotion (null = random) budget stripGraft addGraft addGraftRandom moveGraft shatter queueGraftPick queueFight(run, ids, {elite}) rng strainOf.

## Engine hooks
- A cut is a cut: `seamCut` (relics, hero, enemies) fires when the Line is CUT, never for the turn-end cash. `seamCash` (enemies) fires for every cash (`info.kind` 'cut' or 'end') before payment and may set `info.cancel`. `info.stitches` / `info.pays` are what was PAID: "per stitch" hooks read those, never the Line length. `info.paid` is false past the turn's 3 paying cuts (`SEAM_CUTS_TURN`): relic, hero and per-stitch hooks skip it.
- combat.js calls `seamInit`, `seamStep`, `seamEnd`, `seamTurnStart`, `seamCutNow` (`A.cutLine`). `seamPreview(c, card)` is pure and must equal the real payout (`seam`, `ui_seam`, `rules_fixes` check it); `seamWorth(c)` is what cutting now pays. Declaration (`declare: '<school>'` on a hero, Odile): `canDeclare` / `setDeclare` in seam.js, control `drawSeamDeclare` (key G).
- Resonance: the rule is engine/resonance.js, the texts data/schools.js (`resonanceText`, `RESONANCE_THREAD`); `rules_fixes` measures both, so change them together. A tier is decided only in `resonanceOf(run, kind)` (Resonance Lens: `mod.resLens`).
- `card.claimed = true` (The Matron's Claim) fights and shows like a Tithe that combat; Tempered cards are never claimed; content never sets `tithed`. Afflictions are called from combat.js (`afflStartTurn`, `afflEndTurn`, `afflCost`, `afflAbsorb`).

## Writing and edit rules
- Voice: clinical tenderness. Second person, short, dry, medical with a wink. Prices stated plainly. No exclamation marks, flavor is one line.
- Never rename save keys, the `graftwork/` folder, or card, graft, relic, enemy, room or event ids (saves reference them; a save naming an unknown id drops it and loads).
- Prefer a hook or an exported helper over editing the middle of a shared function; additive changes (a field with a default, an optional op) merge cleanly. Merge conflicts: keep HEAD, re-grep your feature, run the runner.
- Remove code only with proof (`node tools/graftwork_hygiene.mjs`; the allowlist only shrinks and every entry says why in `reasons`).
- After ANY change under `graftwork/`: `node tools/graftwork_sw_build.mjs` (the `sw` suite fails on a stale offline list).

## UI contract
- One 1280x720 logical canvas, immediate mode. Use `app.text/panel/button/tooltip/inRect/clicked/tween/after/wrap/rr`, `COL`, `EASE`; never the DOM. Schools always get a SHAPE glyph as well as a colour. Fonts: Georgia for names and flavor, Trebuchet for small UI.
- Shared state: `app.shared.run`, `.meta`, `.audio`, `.flow`. Screens change the run ONLY through engine/run.js and engine/combat.js, then call `flow.route()` (saves, then switches to the screen of `run.phase`: ward combat reward graft shop forge event, dead/won to end). After a fight `RUN.resolveFight(run, finishCombat(c))`; then `RUN.afterReward`, `RUN.afterGraft`, `RUN.advance` (shop, forge), `RUN.afterEvent`; all go back to the walk. A practice fight is `newRun({ bare: true })`.
- Frame loop and the modal rule (`ui/core.js`, `ui/frame_state.js`): only the TOP screen updates and draws. A screen opened with `app.push` (every modal) freezes everything under it: no update, no draw, no input, no time; the lower stack is painted once into one snapshot (`app.frameState` says when it is stale; a modal can call `app.refreshBelow()`). Time has an owner: use `dt`, `app.t`, `app.tween`, `app.after`, never `setTimeout` or the wall clock. A `draw` must be safe with `dt` 0 and no input. Optional hooks `freeze(app)`, `thaw(app, ms)`, `calm(app)` (30 fps idle). Never frozen: audio, saving, the offline download, the quality controller.
- Layout registry: every HUD widget calls `app.reg(id, x, y, w, h, { layer: 'hud'|'world'|'overlay', group, overlay })` each frame. `hud` rects never intersect unless they share a `group` or one is `overlay`; non-`world` rects stay inside the stage. Text never under `SMALL.minTextPx` (11), touch targets never under `SMALL.minTouchPx` (40) on phones.
- Tooltips (ONE system): a tip exists only while its anchor is hovered, held or focused THIS frame; show delay 0.25 s (touch hold 0.3 s), dropped on a press, Esc, blur, screen change or `app.tipBlock`; Shift (touch: hold 0.9 s) holds it open with definitions and arithmetic. One compact panel (max 38% of the stage) beside the anchor, clear of HUD rects; extra definitions collapse into `+N more (hold)`. New tips: `app.tooltip(title, text, { gloss, anchor, region })` or `queueTip(...)`. Every rules word needs a glossary entry (`glossary`).

## Save keys (never rename)
- `graftwork_run_v1` (engine/save.js): the run in progress, written by `flow.route()`, deleted on death or win. Every run has `mode: 'ward'`; `row`, `node`, `map` stay `-1`, `null`, `null` (unchanged shape). A run save with another or no mode is a retired Classic run (`isRetiredRun`; practice fights are not): `META.retireClassicRun(meta)` closes it like Abandon (`recordRunEnd`), deletes it and sets `meta.notice`.
- `graftwork_meta_v1` (engine/meta.js): base shape `v: 1` is final; additive revisions use `rev` (meta2.js `defaults2`, `migrate2`). `migrate()` fills missing fields: adding one is safe, changing a type is not. Optional `notice` (`'classic_retired'`, shown once from `NOTICES`). A stored `settings.mode` is ignored and left alone.
- Settings defaults live in ONE table, `SETTING_DEFAULTS` (engine/meta.js); `DEFAULT_SETTINGS` (a new meta) is it without the display keys; ui/settings.js `DEFAULTS` and ui/a11y.js `A11Y_DEFAULTS` pick from it. Open: `ASSIST_DEFAULT` (engine/assist.js) disagrees with the table on `gentle`; making them agree is a behaviour decision.

## The Ward (design/WARD.md)
Every run is a Ward run: the hospital you build is the map (the Classic node map is retired). A room the hero walks into opens its screen (`startRoute` / `afterTrigger`; `flow.route()` first shows the walk, `wardInterlude`). Rooms are w x h cells (`room.w`, `room.h`): use `WD.cellsOf(r)`, `WD.neighbors`, `WD.sizeKey`, never `r.x, r.y` as the only cell (WARD.md section 14). The map is isometric (TW 92 x TH 48 world px, `ISO.pick`, `cellCenter` is the SCREEN centre of a floor; section 12). Rooms draw strictly back to front by x + y; each registers `ward-room-<id>` on the `world` layer. Walk pacing is `PACE` in ui/ward.js. Reduced motion removes wander, bob, flicker, motes and the walk.

## Art pipelines
- **Ward art exception.** The owner authorised AI-generated raster art for the Ward only (its actors and kit also appear in battles and the title scene); everything else stays procedural. `assets/ward/` (3.9 MB WebP, budget 12 MB): plot tile, 11 room paintings used as card art, actors (standing figure, `@3x` for battle, optional battle frames `atk1..3 / hit / die`), icons, card art; `@1x` phones, `@2x` desktop. The manifest names exactly the files on disk; the dark look is a code tint. Rebuild: `python3 tools/graftwork_art_build.py` (`--combat`, `--frames`, `--prune [--dry]`).
- **Room kit** (`assets/ward_kit/`, recipes in data/ward_kit.js, WARD.md section 13): rebuild with `python3 tools/graftwork_kit_build.py`. A room is its procedural block until ITS recipe's images have settled (`KR.recipeFor`), then the kit for good (kit images are never evicted).
- **Loading.** Nothing is preloaded: the code that draws an image asks for it and gets null until it is decoded (`ui/assets.js`, a no-op in node). Never keep an `{img}` result across frames.
- **Battles** draw the painted actors (enemies flipped) on a kit backdrop; an id without a sprite keeps the graded procedural figure (WARD.md 12b). **The map walk** is never a frame: `gait()` drives the puppet (`ui/puppet.js`), else the hop.
- **Previews, off for every player**: `previewOn(name, app.settings)` (`ui/preview.js`). `art` = painted UI parts (`assets/ui/`), `rig` = character rigs (`assets/rigs/`); on only with `?art=1`, `?rig=1`, `?preview=1` (`=0` forces off) or Settings > "Painted art (preview)" (`settings.previewArt`, both); `PREVIEW_DEFAULT` would make one the default. While off NOTHING under those folders is requested and frames are unchanged (`ui_art`, `rig`, `perf3`). A painted part falls back to the procedural look in the same rectangle: add it to the build tool, draw it with `SK.nine / SK.part / SK.icon` behind an `if`, old drawing in the `else` (design/ART_MODULAR.md, `lab/art.html`). Rigs: seven of nine ship (`ship: false` = lab only), never in battles, bone ids by SCREEN side (ART_MODULAR.md section 7, ANIMATOR_GUIDE.md section 6, `lab/anim.html?scene=rig`).
- **Rebuild the previews**: `python3 -I tools/graftwork_ui_build.py --raw <dir>`, `python3 -I tools/graftwork_rig_build.py --raw <dir> [--only id] [--fit]` (choices by eye in `tools/graftwork_rig_meta.json`; `--fit` measures how far each limb must reach up into the body, with the poses of `node tools/graftwork_rig_pose.mjs`, and writes it to the meta file as `fit`; a rig can also come as one assembled A-pose figure, `"source": "assembled"`, which the tool slices; `--export <dir>` writes any rig as a spec v2 parts sheet). Before a new sheet is built: `python3 -I tools/graftwork_rig_check.py <sheet.png> [--out <annotated.png>] [--spec]`, the linter of the rig sheet spec (PASS or FAIL per rule, one sentence per failed rule for the artist). **Raw files are never committed**: the repo holds only what the game draws, and the builds are deterministic, so tool plus output is the record. After any rebuild: `node tools/graftwork_sw_build.mjs`.

## Performance and quality tiers (ui/quality.js, design/PERF.md)
Settings > Graphics (`auto` | `high` | `low`) drives `app.perf.q.tier` (`full | lite | minimal`, Auto measures tick intervals). Read the tier, never time JS: below `full` there is no WebGL post, no motes, low smoothing, `@1x` sprites, a lower `app.perf.dprCap`. `app.perf.gov` idles calm screens at 30 fps; `?perf` shows a readout. Decoded images are budgeted and evicted LRU (`maintain` in assets.js), except the room kit. Measure with `node tools/graftwork_profile.mjs` (no GPU in the sandbox: compare ratios).

## Test map
`node tests/run_graftwork.mjs` (`npm run test:graftwork`) runs the 73 suites on 4 workers (`--jobs=N`, `--serial`, `--times`), slowest first (times kept in the system temp folder, else `HEAVY`). On a shared machine compare CPU seconds and re-run a failing suite alone first. A suite counts a budget (`canvasCalls()`), never times it, never asserts on source text, leaves dashes to `hygiene`, and is named after what it guards. Reach a screen through the Ward (`wardOpen(run, 'fight' | 'elite' | 'boss' | 'event' | 'shop' | 'forge')`).
| area | suites |
|---|---|
| content gate (run after every data edit) | data, content_fuzz, audit, status_behaviour, hygiene (dead code, duplicate helpers, orphan assets, dashes, the module map above, tool argument guards) |
| rules | temper, seam, afflictions, boss_mechanics, intents, meta, unlock, assist, rules_fixes (text equals rule), difficulty |
| whole runs | regression (strong-bot runs, round trips, the Ward save fixtures resume to the same end), e2e (real boot and flow), retire (Classic saves closed and counted, the notice) |
| combat UI | ui_combat, ui_combat_layout (1..6 enemies x 0..10 cards), ui_combat_art, ui_juice, ui_seam, motion, ui_transitions |
| the Ward | ward, ward_big, ward_props, ui_ward, ui_ward_kit |
| other screens | ui_event, ui_graft, ui_shop, ui_deck, ui_title, ui_pause, ui_archive, onboarding, ui_layout (`OPEN_WARD` lists known faults) |
| tooltips | tip_lifecycle, tip_layout (every tip through `checkTip`), glossary |
| input, a11y, frame loop | touch, a11y, monkey, ui_core, phone_display, frozen (screens under a modal) |
| art | art_cards, art_chars, art_icons, attack_rigs, grade, linework, templates |
| Ward art, loading, previews | art_assets (manifests equal the disk), ward_loading (exactly what a Ward asks for), anim_frames, puppet, lab_anim, rig, rig_check (the sheet linter), rig_limbs (long hidden limbs, the assembled A-pose slicer), ui_art |
| fx, audio, perf | fx, post, audio, audio_mix, perf2, perf3, quality |
| offline and install | sw, install |
Suites that need Chromium (`post`, `grade`, `audio_mix`, the page checks of `rig`, `puppet`, `lab_anim`) skip that part with a message when Playwright is missing; build-tool checks skip without Python or Pillow. To LOOK: `python3 -m http.server 8123` from the repo root, Playwright Chromium on `http://localhost:8123/graftwork/` (`window.__gw.app`, `.flow`).

## Tools
```
node tools/graftwork_ward_sim.mjs 60 0 strong     # per-act table (or `all`, `both`, a policy)
node tools/graftwork_strong.mjs 40 0 [hero]       # strong bot (Thread aware): the reference for win rates
node tools/graftwork_sim.mjs 20 0 [policy ...]    # graft policies (default careful greedy none)
node tools/graftwork_tiers.mjs                    # skill tiers random / sloppy / careful / strong (targets: design/DIFFICULTY.md)
node tools/graftwork_schools.mjs schools 100 0    # mono-school builds; also pairs, relics [N] [asc] [relicId]
node tools/graftwork_audit.mjs 40 --tier=weird    # per-graft paired comparison (--pool, --graft, --rows)
node tools/graftwork_cardsweep.mjs marrow 200 0   # each card as 2 copies in the starter deck versus a control
node tools/graftwork_unlocks.mjs 200 30           # unlock pacing (design/UNLOCKS.md)
node tools/graftwork_hygiene.mjs                  # the hygiene report (--list, --json, --prune-allow, --unexplained)
node tools/graftwork_golden.mjs record out.json   # golden master (engine, resume, draw hashes; then compare)
node tools/graftwork_profile.mjs                  # tick cost in real Chromium (writes design/PERF_RESULT.json)
node tools/graftwork_audio_check.mjs [--json]     # offline loudness, clipping, masking (Chromium)
node tools/graftwork_glossary_audit.mjs           # every glossary definition, for a read-through
node tools/graftwork_sw_build.mjs [--check]       # rewrite (or check) sw-manifest.json
python3 tools/graftwork_icons.py                  # the four PWA icons, byte for byte
```
A tool that writes committed files prints usage for `--help` and writes nothing for an unknown option (`hygiene` checks). Bots live in `tools/graftwork_driver.mjs` (`playWardRun(hero, seed, policy, asc, opts)`; policies strong careful greedy none sloppy random passive fill); its frozen tuning constants are at the top of the section that uses them. Environment switches (defaults are the published numbers):
| switch | read by | effect |
|---|---|---|
| `GW_UNLOCK=all\|start\|start+kind,kind`, `GW_POOL=start` | driver, ward_sim; unlocks | card pool: all (default), the starting pool, or it plus every locked item of those kinds |
| `GW_TUNE='{...}'`, `GW_WEIRD_K` | driver | patch Ward numbers in memory (what-if); credit of custom-hook grafts in the strong pick (0.5) |
| `GW_PICK=0` `GW_EXPAND=0` `GW_DECLARE=0` `GW_HOLDCUT=0` `GW_EARLY=0` `GW_OLDWALK=1` | driver | strong bot buys no cards / never Expands / never declares / plays "Cut the Line." at once / no early boss / the pre-dice walker |
| `GW_SLIP` `GW_TAG` | driver, tiers | careful misplay rate (0.09); another seed batch |
| `GW_REG_SEEDS` `GW_MONKEY_RUNS` `GW_MONKEY_TICKS` `GW_MONKEY_SEED=hero:index` `GW_FUZZ_SEED` `GW_FUZZ_VERBOSE=1` `RF_ALL=1` | regression, monkey, content_fuzz, rules_fixes | more seeds or ticks, replay one seed, list advisories, list every failing block |
Balance targets (design/WARD.md, DIFFICULTY.md): strong bot all pool 25 to 40 percent per hero, spread at most 12, read at N = 500 or more (N = 60 has a 6 point standard deviation); random never wins, sloppy almost never (`difficulty`). Last measured (rules follow-ups, N = 500): 31 / 30 / 32 percent Marrow / Wick / Odile. A school or relic that moves the strong bot by more than about 10 points is an outlier.
**Verify:** `node tests/graftwork_data.test.mjs` after content edits, `node tools/graftwork_sw_build.mjs --check`, then `node tests/run_graftwork.mjs` before handing back. Never run other games' suites or `npm run check`.
