# ENEMY DEPTH SPEC (council memo, systems designer)

Engine facts: player 'dur' statuses tick at the player's endTurn BEFORE enemies act, so dur 1 applied in the enemy phase covers exactly the next player turn. Player status hooks that exist: turnStart, turnEnd, cardPlayed, damaged, attacked. ID COLLISIONS: 'fragile' (Cherub +50% taken), 'marked', frail, corroded, costup, drawdown, lured, priced, tally already exist, so the player Fragile idea is named BRITTLE. Statuses marked (E) need a small engine hook; the rest are status-file only (data/statuses_e1/e2/e3.js).

## 1. New statuses (player unless noted; tooltip = the quoted line)
1. Poison (int, cap 6): "Lose N HP at turn start (ignores Block), then N rises by 1." Max +2 per move. Cleansed by cleanse; halved (round up) when its applier dies. Icon: green droplet with rising tick.
2. Brittle (dur, cap 3): "Block you gain shatters: when an attack empties your Block, take 25% of the broken amount (max 6)." Counter: overblock or kill the hitter. Icon: cracked shield.
3. Rejection (dur 1, E): "Prices trigger twice this turn (self damage, junk, jam)." Applied only by hits of 12+. Counter: play bare or fused cards. Icon: stitch pulled from cloth.
4. Infection (int, cap 3, E): "Strain counts +N this combat." Tithes the next Bare card per point; with no Bare card it adds Backfire. Cleared by Rest/Forge or potions only. Icon: yellow seam.
5. Numb (dur 1, E): "The Boons of your top grafted card in hand are muted this turn." Pick the highest-weight card in hand. Icon: gray patch.
6. Unpicked (dur 1, E): "One patch on one card is gone this turn." Extends Stitch Rat theft to a single graft on any card in hand. Overload cards drop to 2 grafts for the turn (no x1.5, no Shatter roll). Icon: loose thread.
7. Cadence Lock (dur 1, E, sequencing): breaks the new school Chain on the first repeated school (exact wording follows the final Chain/rule API). Icon: broken link.
8. Stagefright (int, cap 4): "Each card past your 4th this turn adds 1 to the damage of the next enemy hit." Resets at your turn start.
9. Dread (int, cap 5): "If you end a turn with no Block, take 2 per stack." Passive-play punisher.
10. Leaden (dur 2): "Your highest-cost card costs +1."
11. Weight of Debt (int, enemy only, cap 8): "Gains +1 per card type you skip; its next hit +2 per stack."
12. Stitched Up (int, enemy, cap 10): "Absorbs the first N damage of each turn; refills if you play fewer than 3 cards."
Caps: at most 2 new player debuffs per enemy move; at most 3 distinct new debuff types active on the player at once (extras refresh instead of stacking).

## 2. Damage tuning (extra average damage/turn, spread over the fight)
Act1 baseline 9 add +1.5 (target 10.5; 50% conditional, 30% scaling, 20% telegraph). Act2 13 add +2.5 (15.5; 40/30 DoT+scaling/30 telegraph). Act3 19 add +4 (23; 30/30/40 telegraph+multi-turn). Conditional hooks: block under 5 (Dread), 5+ cards (Ledgerman, Stagefright), one card dealing 20+ (Scale Merchant mirror), no attack played (Hush +50% next hit). Conditional extras only fire if the player CHOSE the behaviour; against balanced play Act 1 stays near 9.5. Telegraphed bigs follow a two-turn cycle (Charging status). Any single telegraphed hit at most 3.0x act baseline.

## 4. AI rules
Reactive pick(): enemies read c.player.block, c.strain.strain, last card school and Chain state only at end of the player's turn; reactions are a choice between 2 PREPARED moves, never an invented move. Several enemies: guard role (Stitched Up/Block) acts first, buffer second, scaler last. One Dread-style punisher per group. Readability: always show intent AND number; hidden intents only on Night Nurse and the Cleaver chop (tell visible), at most one hidden enemy per fight; every conditional bonus is listed in the tooltip ("+4 if you hold 3+ junk"); the intent number INCLUDES Chain and Dread bonuses.

## 5. Guardrails and test plan
Caps: Poison 6, Infection 3, Brittle 3, Dread 5, enemy Strength 10, 2 new debuffs per move. Cleanse removes Poison and Brittle but not Infection. Edge cases: Poison cannot kill below 1 HP if a wouldDie relic is untriggered; Infection with 0 Bare cards flows into Backfire (capped by deck size); Unpicked on an Overload card also skips its Shatter roll that turn; Unpicked and Stitch Rat must not hit the same card twice. Sim: `node tools/graftwork_sim.mjs 100 0` before and after for careful/greedy/none; targets HP lost per fight +12 to +18% per act (Act 1 elite at most +20%), win rate drop at most 8 points careful / 5 none at ascension 0, greedy must still lose more than none; add a per-enemy damage-by-turn histogram; smoke ascension 5 and 10 for caps; add an assertion test that every new status resolves in STATUSES and every new move has valid intent kinds.
