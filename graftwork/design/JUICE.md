# GRAFTWORK ANIMATION AND GAME FEEL (council memo, animation head)

## Critique of current feel
Every enemy attacks the same way: combat.js feeds one `attack` scalar (0..1..0 over 500 ms) to every body; no wind-up; bodies that read A.att < 0 never fire. Lunge peaks at 200 ms but the dmg event lands at 290 ms. Hits scale by raw numbers (hit-stop at 6 and 12, crit = n > 15) so in Act 3 everything is a crit and in Act 1 nothing is. Shake is directionless noise. Multi-hits (pill_swarm up to 10) spawn 10 overlapping floats and shakes. Float physics depend on frame rate. Burn and poison ticks feel the same. No low-HP danger state. Played card lingers at 0.34 scale until 1.4 s. Exhaust is a fading orange rectangle. Endings are thin; defeat shows no cause.

## Animation language
TELL, ACT, HOLD, RECOVER. Light: 120/60/40/180 ms. Heavy: 280/70/90/320. Boss: 450 + telegraph glint/90/140/400. Contact happens at the END of ACT and the damage event must land on that frame.
Easing: backIn(1.7) tells, expoOut strikes, elasticOut(amp 1, period 0.3) settles, sineInOut idle, critically damped spring for followers. Squash and stretch preserve volume (scaleX = 1/scaleY): tell squash 0.85 along the strike axis, strike stretch 1.25, victim squash 0.8 for 60 ms then spring k=380 damping 18; jars and slime max 1.3; metal max 1.08 (hard things barely deform).
Secondary motion (one damped spring per appendage, per-enemy state blob in art/enemies.js): cloth hems lag 80 ms; cables (defibrillator paddles, iron_lung hose) are 6-point Verlet ropes; jar brine surface tilts with x acceleration, 0.4 s slosh; candle flames lean against velocity and gutter 200 ms on hit.
Patchwork creatures: each patch is a rigid piece joined by a spring to its neighbour; stitch lines drawn between anchors, thread thins and reddens as tension rises; at 50% HP one seam POPS (thread whips free, tick sound, patch sags 4 px thereafter). Death reuses PIECES unstitch.
ATTACK RIGS: add `ATTACKS` keyed by enemy id with a body fallback: `{ tell, act, hold, rec, impactMs, pose(phase, k) }`. combat.js passes `atkPhase`/`atkK` instead of the scalar; the enemyAct handler returns tell+act as its wait so dmg lands on contact. Engine untouched.
Ten signatures: bedpan_slime squashes flat 200 ms then leaps in an arc and splats (dome 1.4 wide, droplets). orderly raises the clipboard overhead (300 ms tell), slams and stamps (ink-blot decal on hero 1 s). iron_lung inhales (scale 1.15, ribs spread, hose tightens) then blows (streaks, hero pushed back 20 px). pill_swarm capsules orbit faster each hit and peel off one by one. stitch_rat tail whips, the thread drags a ghost of the stolen patch into its body. night_nurse lamp goes dark 250 ms (screen dims 30%), syringe stabs from the dark. defibrillator flatlines the screen, paddles rub with sparks; Clear = 120 ms white flash and the hero silhouette stiffens. the_matron bell rears 450 ms, candle flames streak, swings like a pendulum, low bell strike, horizontal shake. cleaver tilts back and holds with a glint on Raise; Chop falls in 50 ms and freezes 120 ms blade embedded. the_grafter stretches a limb made of YOUR graft patches toward you, stitches visibly strain and snap back.

## Hit-feel table (tiers relative to victim max HP: light < 8%, medium 8 to 18%, heavy > 18%; crit = Vulnerable applied or > 30%; overkill = damage beyond remaining HP)
light: stop 0, shake 3 px decay 8/s, no flash, 6 sparks, 28 px vellum number hop 40 px.
medium: stop 45, shake 6 px directional decay 6/s, victim white 60 ms, 12 sparks + ring, 36 px number pop 1.4.
heavy: stop 90, shake 11 px + 1.5 deg roll decay 5/s, white 90 ms, CA 3 px, 20 + dust, 48 px number with ink splatter behind.
crit: stop 120, shake 14 px decay 4/s, red rim, CA 6 px, zoom 1.04, 26 + shards, 58 px red number tilted 8 deg slamming down.
overkill: stop 140, shake 16, CA 8 px, slow 0.4 for 250 ms, gore confetti, "+N OVERKILL" gold small.
block: stop 20, shake 3, blue edge, 8 rivets, grey flat number. block-break: stop 70, shake 9, plate cracks into 4 shards, 14 metal particles, "BROKEN" stamp.
poison tick: green pulse, 5 rising bubbles, small green number sinks. burn tick: shake 2, orange flicker, 8 embers up, small orange wobbling number. heal: soft green bloom, 12 motes drifting up. self-damage: stop 60, shake 5, red seam on the paying card, 6 blood, red number with a stitch mark.
Sound layers on every hit: transient (noise tick), body (pitched thump by tier), tail (reverb send by tier).
Multi-hit counter: hits on the same target within 350 ms merge into ONE number that ticks up (grows 4% per hit, shakes harder, rising pitch) and slams "x6 = 42" on the last hit. First blood: first hit of a combat gets +30 ms hit-stop and a quill-scratch sound.
FINISHING BLOW (last kill only): hold 140 ms; slow-mo 0.25 for 600 ms, zoom 1.12 on the victim, CA 8 px; seams light up one by one (60 ms each with ticks) then unstitch; shards scatter and a lone thread floats down. BOSS KILL adds 1 s of music silence, desaturation draining the boss colour from the background, a heart-thump then a choir swell.
HERO DEATH (2.8 s, skippable after 1 s): killing hit freezes 200 ms; the intent of the killer stays lit and labelled "Killed by Stamp (11)"; seams split, patches slump, heartbeat slows over 3 beats and stops; fade to vellum; last line "The ward remembers."
VICTORY (1.8 s): hero flourish idle (stretch 1.1 then settle); banner stamps in with a 90 ms hold; gold arcs into the counter one plink per coin rising pentatonic; reward preview slides up.

## Card choreography (ms)
Draw: fan out 70 apart, backOut, pitch +1 semitone per card, 2-degree overshoot settle. Play: lift 90 (scale 1.1), wind-up 70 (20 px back, 0.9 squash), flight 110 on a quadratic arc with a school-coloured ribbon trail, impact 40 (card flattens 0.6 against the target), fold 150 to discard. Discard: 260 arc, 40 apart. Exhaust: 450 dissolve burning from the bottom up on a noise threshold, glowing burnt edge, ash flakes. Shatter: crack 120, hold 80, burst into gold-edged shards, slow 0.35 for 350 ms, reversed cymbal. Tithe: red seam crosses in 220, numbers flicker to the -25% values, dry snap. Graft attach: patch slides 150, needle zigzags 6 x 45 ms with ticks, staple 60 ms clunk, 40 ms freeze. Temper: card heats orange 200, hammer 3 strikes x 140 each with sparks and 30 ms stop, rivets pop 80, cools to steel blue 400. Jam: clamp snaps 80, card shakes 200 with grinding stutter, "+1 cost" rubber stamp.

## Addiction loops
Combo meter: damage dealt this turn builds a counter beside the hero, changing colour at 20, 40, 80 with rising pitch; at end of turn it cashes out with a stamp. Low HP: below 30% a heartbeat at 60 BPM rising to 110 below 15%, vignette pulsing in time, music lowpassed to 800 Hz. Near-miss: surviving at 5 HP or less plays an "it held" stinger. Turn end: block plates unbolt, cards sweep away in one wave, a wood knock as the enemy turn begins. Rewards: cards arrive face down; each flip has a 250 ms tell, rares hold 150 ms more and shimmer. Level-ups: wax seal pressed then a bell. Micro-sounds: hovering a playable card = paper rustle; a non-playable one = muted tick.
