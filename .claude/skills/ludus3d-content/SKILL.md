# Ludus3D content: the fallen, rival ludi, calendar, barracks

Files: `core/ext.js` (pure sim, loaded after `core/sim.js` in index.html, hooked by one line in `LM.weekFinish`: `if (LM.extWeek) LM.extWeek(S, rep)`), `js/screens/world.js` (screen id `world`, tabs calendar / rivals / barracks / memorial, opened with `ctx.open('world', {tab})`), `js/flow.js` (`mortalCheck`, `fallenFlow`, `heirOffer`), `js/screens/modes.js` (risk modes), `js/ceremonies.js` (`eulogy` kind `fallen`). Tests: rounds 40 to 42 in `tests/ludus_maximus_3d.test.mjs` (the test requires ext.js after sim.js) and block 10 at the end of `tests/ludus_maximus_3d_structure.test.mjs`. The 2D game in `ludus_maximus/` is untouched and has none of this. No em dashes, no new localStorage keys (all state lives on the save object S, key `ludus_maximus_3d_v1`).

## Philosophy
No counter mechanics. Nothing here punishes a fighting style. Risk is explicit (a "Death risk N%" pill before you commit), buildings soften it (Infirmary -10% per level, Shrine -8% per level, laurel Luck perk), and every loss has a payoff (fame, an heir, a memorial). Hardcore is opt-in.

## 1. The fallen
- `LM.mortality(S, g, {kind, depth, boss})` is the chance (0..0.6) that a fighter who LOST dies. Base per kind: pit .07 (+.035 per floor beyond 1), horde .06 (+.025 per wave), champ .08, bounty .10, event .04 (events are 0 unless `S.opts.hardcore`). Boss +.08. Hardcore x1.35. Lucky trait x.75. Level 1 and 2 fighters on pit floors 1 and 2 are spared outside Hardcore. `LM.deathRoll` rolls with the sim RNG and returns `{dies, chance}`.
- `LM.fall(S, g, {kind, depth, by})` does everything: memorial entry in `S.fallen` (cap 60), gear back to the armory (via `LM.release`, which also withdraws entries), mentor pairs ended, grief (`mo -= 6 + bond*0.2`, `g.grief` 3 weeks for bond >= 50, 1 for >= 25; the weekly hook costs 3 morale per grief week), the closest friend (bond >= 30) inherits `+ceil(lvl/4)` of the fallen's best stat and a `vow`, fame `8 + 2*lvl + wins/2`, `S.heirPending` for level 3+. Returns `{entry, grief[], inherited, heir, fame}`.
- Heirs: `LM.takeHeir(S)` adds a free level `floor(lvl/3)` pupil of the same style with 3 talent pips in the fallen's best stat and the title "Heir of X" (needs barracks room, else 'Barracks full'); `LM.monument(S)` gives `15 + 4*lvl` fame instead. Legacy: `LM.legacyFame(S)` = min(10, 2 per fallen of level 5+, +1 per level 10+) fame each week, reported in the week summary. The memorial survives `LM.ascend`.
- UI: modes.js rolls after a Pit or Horde defeat (the fighter is removed BEFORE the report so no xp or injury is applied), `flow.mortalCheck(ctx, gl, res, {kind})` does it for champion duels, bounties and, in `runEvent` / `runTourney`, ordinary events (zero risk unless Hardcore; only fighters whose unit went `down`). `fallenFlow` shows the eulogy ceremony (light text: the scrim is dark) then `heirOffer`. More > Settings > Play has "Hardcore (permadeath)" (default off). Arena cards show a death-risk pill in the fighter picker for Pit, Horde, champion and bounty.
- Pitfall: results/report code reads `ctx.gOf(id)` for every fighter; never delete a fighter before `results()` has run. The fall happens after the report dialog.

## 2. Rival ludi (`S.ludi`, created lazily and deterministically from `S.seed` by `LM.ludiInit`)
Four schools, one of each personality (`LM.PERS`): brutal (gifts barely move him, sparring in his yard does; hostile ones ambush a fighter), cunning (flattery works, rumors get traced; hostile ones steal gold), honorable (joint training best; never raids; remembers insults), greedy (gifts work wonders; spurned ones poach your best recruit). Regard `rel` is -100..100 and drifts to 0. Actions: `LM.ludiAct(S, id, 'gift' | 'spar' | 'rumor' | 'ally' | 'break', g)` returns `{msg, rel}` or an error string; `LM.ludiWhy(S, id, kind, g)` explains why not ('' means allowed). One action per kind per ludus per week. Alliance needs regard 60 and gold, one at a time, pays a weekly gift (brutal fame, cunning scouting +1, honorable morale and energy, greedy gold) and ends below regard 40. Standings: `LM.ludiTable(S)` (rivals gain points weekly, you score 3 per win and 6 per title); at season end the champion is recorded in `S.ludi.hist` (+fame if you win) and scores reset.

## 3. Calendar
`LM.calendar(S)` is pure and cheap (no opponent generation): 12 weeks of the current season with festival, big event (`tourney` every 4th week, `grand` every 12th), team clash (week % 4 == 2), your entries (from `S.entries` keys) and a weather forecast. The World screen tile grid opens a detail card and the Arena at the right week offset (`ctx.open('arena', {tab:'sched', wk})`).

## 4. Barracks
Mentors: `LM.mentorStart(S, mentorId, pupilId)` needs the mentor 3 levels above; slots `1 + Sparring Ring + Barracks level 3`; weekly `8 + mentor level` XP to the pupil, bond +5, both +2 morale; graduates when the pupil reaches mentor level - 1 (+10 fame). Feasts: `LM.feast(S, 'simple' | 'banquet')` once a week (+6 / +12 morale, banquet +8 energy, bonds, eases grief).

## 5. Wounds and scars (core/ext.js wave 2, `js/screens/infirmary.js`, `js/screens/wounds_ui.js`)
- Data: `D.WOUNDS` (gash, ribs, arm, knee, head, eye), `D.SCARS`, `D.TREATS`. A fighter has `g.wounds [{id, part, w, w0, tr, rest}]`; **`g.inj` stays the longest wound in weeks**, so every old screen, the entry checks and the old tests keep working. `LM.syncWounds(S, g)` repairs the list when old code sets or lowers `g.inj` (brawls, visitors, tests); it is called by the weekly hook and by the Infirmary.
- Injury: the wrapped `LM.settle` turns each `rep.inj` entry into a wound (`LM.woundGive`): the part is a hash roll (`LM.woundPart`, heavier eye/head/arm tails when the fighter ended under 25% HP), weeks = the old 1 to 3 times the part multiplier. `rep.inj[i]` gains `part, wid, name`. Hash rolls are keyed on `S.seed`, so old seeded tests see the same sim RNG stream.
- Healing: the wrapped `LM.weekTrain` heals every wound by the Infirmary rate (1 + floor(v), plus 1 with the temple patron), plus 1 more for a wound on **strict bed rest** (`wd.rest`, -3 morale a week). When a wound closes (`LM.closeWound`) it may leave a scar (`LM.scarChance`: part base x length x plan x bed rest x (1 - 0.14 per Infirmary level), lucky trait x0.75, never twice, max 5 scars) and always a short weakness `g.weak {w, mods}` (1 week, 2 for long wounds) that lowers derived stats.
- Decision (Treat or Rest): `LM.treatWound(S, g, wd, 'pou' | 'doc' | 'surg')`, `LM.setRest`, `LM.treatWhy`, `LM.treatOdds` (shows the scar chance after each plan), `LM.treatCost`. Poultice 60% saves a week; physician saves 2 and scar x0.45; surgeon (wounds of 4+ weeks) saves 1, scar x0.12, one time in four goes wrong (+2 weeks, -10 morale, scar x1.5). The old `LM.treat(S, g, 'pou' | 'doc')` still works on the longest wound.
- **Scar ids for the rig and gladiator agents** (`g.scars` is an array of these, `D.SCARS[id].look = {where, kind, side}` says where to draw it): `gash_scar` (face slash, left; +6% fame, -2% dodge), `old_ribs` (torso welt; -5% health, +3% damage), `stiff_arm` (right arm wrap; -5% damage, +4% armor), `bad_knee` (left leg brace; -6% speed, +3% armor), `ringing` (head dent; +5% health, -3% dodge), `lost_eye` (eye patch, left; +4% damage, -5% dodge, -2% crit, +4% fame), `burn` (right arm burn; -3% armor, +4% fame; only from the sabotage event), `whip` (back lashes; -2% speed, +3% damage; reserved for story beats). Every scar costs something. Derived mods are applied by the wrapped `LM.derive` (hp/atk/def/spd multiply, crit/eva add), so fights, odds and previews all see them. `LM.bodyMods(g)`, `LM.modText(mods)`, `LM.scarFame(g)`. Memorial entries (`S.fallen[i].scars`) and legends (`S.legends[i].scars`) keep the list.
- UI: Infirmary tab of The World (and `ctx.open('infirmary', {id})`): per wound a body icon, weeks meter, scar risk pill, bed rest toggle and three treatment buttons with live odds. Every fighter picker shows wound, weakness and scar pills (`woundBadges` in `sk.js`), the roster shows the body part, the profile has a wound card with scar risk and a Treat or rest button. `flow.js` calls `infirmary.woundCall(ctx)` after the week: a decision dialog for fresh wounds of 2+ weeks (first action is "Let them rest").

## 6. Patrons (core/ext.js wave 2, `js/screens/sponsors.js`)
- `D.SPONSORS`: `wine` (Vinarius, appearances and wins, free wine +2 morale a week, fame penalty), `senator` (Corvus, win rate and no deaths, +2 Senate standing a week, Senate penalty), `temple` (Livia, mercy and no deaths, extra wound healing, morale penalty), `smith` (Brennus, wins by a style you own, a gear piece when kept, gold clawback), `veil` (silent partner of a rival, appearances and win rate, scouting +1, rival sneers and fame penalty). Fame gates them (0, 60, 30, 90, 160). `D.SPBEATS[sid]` has three story beats each (one per contract, queued in week 2 of the contract).
- State `S.spons {active, offers, done, rel, cool, hist, beatQ, seq}`. `LM.sponsorOffer(S, sid)` creates an offer with two term sets (steady, bold), `LM.sponsorAccept(S, offerId, 'steady' | 'bold')`, `LM.sponsorDecline`, `LM.sponsorDemand(S, c, d)` -> `{text, got, need, ok, failed}`, `LM.sponsorNote` (called by the wrapped settle), `LM.sponsorWeek` (weekly hook: pay, perks, beats, end of contract, new offers 30% a week). Demands: wins, appear, style, spare, winrate (needs a minimum number of fights), nodeath (a fall breaks it at once). All kept: bonus + fame + goodwill + a renewal offer on better terms; half kept: lukewarm; less: penalty per patron, the patron leaves 6 weeks. Slots: 1, +1 at the Colosseum, +1 at the Imperial Games (`LM.sponsorSlots`); the money follows the best fighter's level (`LM.patronTier`), not fame alone, so a fame farm in the Pit does not get Colosseum stipends.
- Numbers: stipend 7 / 15 / 32 / 70 gold a week by tier times the patron factor (0.8 to 1.6), bonus 1.0x (steady) or 1.2x (bold) of the stipends, so a contract is worth about two weeks of its stipend per week of work, 5 to 15% of a competent player's income. The old one-month merchant event (`S.sponsor`) is kept for old saves.
- UI: Patrons tab of The World: offers with both terms side by side, active contracts with demand meters, the five houses with goodwill, history. Portraits are hand-drawn SVG glyphs (`glyph(sid)`). After the week `sponsors.patronFlow(ctx)` shows beats (choices run `LM.applyFx`) and a notice for fresh offers (first action is "Think it over").

## 7. The event deck and the Chronicle (core/ext.js wave 2, `D.EVDECK` in data.js, `js/screens/events.js`)
- 58 events: the 12 old ones (kept, now named and logged) and 46 new ones as data in `D.EVDECK`: `{id, n, tag, wk (earliest week), cd (cooldown), w (weight), if [conditions], who, t, follow, swap, c: [{l, h (risk hint), cost [base, perLeague], o: [{p, r, fx}]}]}`. Outcomes are rolled in order (`p` is a number or a named function: pguard, pchamp, pchamp2, pbet) and the last one has no `p`. Tokens `{who} {rival} {champ} {pat} {fallen}` are resolved when the event is rolled (`LM.evPrepare`, stored in `S.evCtx`).
- Ops (`LM.applyFx`): gold, fame (`[base, perLeague]`), mo, en, moW, enW, xp, xpYoung, stat, hurt (part, weeks, chance), bond, fac, q (queue a follow-up), flag, clear, status (plague), sp (patron goodwill), ludi, recruit, item, desert (run or free), offer (a patron), heal, scar, bet, payBet, chance. Conditions: two, room, wounded, poor, fallen, scarred, unhappy, lvl4, vet, hostile, fame:N, ml:N, f:key, nf:key, nsp:sid.
- Chains (consequences across weeks, `S.evQ`, `LM.evThreads`): plague rumour (quarantine, herbs, ignore) -> the quiet ending or the cough; the rope on the east wall (talk, guard, ignore) -> over the wall (stand in the way, open the gate, raise the alarm) -> a freed man comes back; a visiting champion -> rematch or praise; a banquet -> the Senator remembers (a patron offer); dogs stop barking (night watch, search, nothing) -> fire at dawn, or the arsonists caught when you posted a watch (`swap`); a bookmaker -> settles next week; a veteran's promise of freedom -> due in 6 weeks -> he returns. Follow events never roll on their own.
- Roll: from week 3, due follow-ups first, else 40% a week (26% right after an event), weighted among eligible events (week, condition, cooldown). `S.evLog` (80), `S.evHist` (id -> last week), `S.evFlags`, `S.evStatus` (plague: -10 energy and -2 morale a week). `S.evLast {r, fx}` carries the result chips for the dialog. `flow.pendingEvent` shows the named event with hints on each button and effect chips on the result. Chronicle tab: statuses, threads in motion, a filterable log, and a codex (stories met of 58).

## 8. Balance harness (`tools/ludus3d_balance.mjs`)
`node tools/ludus3d_balance.mjs --careers 60 --weeks 96 [--seed N] [--hardcore] [--json out]` plays seeded careers on core/*.js (no DOM): hires, builds, buys gear, plans, enters level-matched events, treats wounds, accepts patrons and answers events at random, then prints gold and fame by season, win and injury rate by league and kind, wound parts and scars, deaths (Hardcore), income by source and patron activity. The policy is level-matched and does not use augments, tricks or drills, so win rates are a floor, not a forecast; use it as a regression and outlier detector. Round 46 of the sim test has quick guardrails (patron money against purses, injury rate near the old mix, scars rare).

Pitfalls: `LM.settle`, `LM.weekTrain`, `LM.derive`, `LM.treat`, `LM.rollEvent`, `LM.runEvent`, `LM.fall`, `LM.retire`, `LM.extWeek` and `LM.migrate` are wrapped in the second IIFE at the end of ext.js; other agents append earlier sections of ext.js, keep the wrapper block last. The new screens scope their CSS to `.lx-dep` and inject it themselves (`injectDepCss`). Dialog buttons the bot clicks first must be harmless: wound call "Let them rest", patron call "Think it over", beat first choices are mild.

## Depth audit: 2D versus 3D (what was missing)
The 3D sim is a copy of the 2D sim, so every system exists; the gaps were presentation and loops. Implemented: the fallen with Hardcore (neither game could kill), rival ludi (the 2D game only had one rival owner), season calendar, mentors and feasts. Wave 2 closed the injury, sponsor and event gaps (sections 5 to 7). Still weak or missing: scars are data only until rig.js and gladiator.js read `g.scars`, patrons do not negotiate beyond steady or bold, event choices have no art, wounds are not shown on the 3D fighter, there is no way to fight hurt (a wounded fighter simply cannot enter), the rival owner champion is a one-off, no in-fight camera for kill replays beyond the director cue.

## MOVE: rearranging the buildings (core/ext.js fourth wrapper block, D.MOVE in data.js)
Owner request: "Would be cool if you could move your buildings. Even if it would cost something." Tests: round 49 of `tests/ludus_maximus_3d.test.mjs` and block 14 of the structure test. No counter mechanics: moving costs gold and a short downtime, nothing else.
- API: `LM.moveQuote(S, from, to)` -> `{ok, why, kind: 'move'|'swap', gold, weeks: [a, b], d, fx[], big}`, `LM.moveBuilding(S, from, to, {busy})` -> `''` or the reason (an empty `to` moves, a built `to` swaps), `LM.moveUndo(S, plot)` / `LM.moveUndoInfo`, `LM.moving(S, i)` (the construction record while `S.week < mv.u`), `LM.moveWhySource`, `LM.moveWhyDest`, `LM.moveEffects` (neighbour synergy changes, read from position so adjacency is always live), `LM.moveDist` (Manhattan on the 4 x 4 grid), `LM.moveValue` (gold put in over all levels).
- Price: `max(20, round5(value * 0.07 * (0.65 + 0.35 * d) * big * lv5))` with `big` x1.4 for the Stands and Sparring Ring (crowd-facing, arena side) and `lv5` x1.5 for level 5. Swap: 40% of both moves together. Downtime `clamp(1 + (level >= 3) + (level >= 5) + (d >= 4), 1, 3)` weeks, a swap one week less for each building (min 1). Undo in the same week returns 75%. Examples: Kitchen L1 one plot 20 gold 1 week; Forge L3 across the yard about 125 to 190 gold 2 to 3 weeks; Forge L5 one plot about 740 gold 3 weeks; Stands L5 about 1 500 gold.
- Pinned (`D.MOVE.pinned`): the Hall of Legends (consecrated statues and the memorial). It also cannot take part in a swap. Refused too: a plot under construction, locked land, the same plot, short gold, `opts.busy` (a fight or event in progress, the UI passes `game.busy`) and `S.pendingEv`.
- Save: only an optional `mv {f: other plot, s: start week, u: ready week, w: weeks, c: gold paid, x: 1 if swap}` on the plot object. No `SAVE_VER` bump (a missing `mv` means finished); the `LM.migrate` wrapper drops garbage or expired records and neutralises a bad `f`. `S.stats.moves` and `S.stats.moveGold` count. The weekly hook (`LM.extWeek` wrapper) drops finished records and adds the note "The crew has finished the X in its new place".
- While under construction: `LM.bfx` returns level 0 (so every perk, the forge cap, kitchen menu, scout level and so on pause), `LM.synAt` gives 0 to it and ignores it as a partner, `LM.upgrade` refuses, `LM.rosterCap` keeps the level 1 bed count (tents) so nobody is evicted. The building keeps its level.

# BATTLE PLAN: roles, formations, priority target, Showmanship (pre-fight strategy)

The old pre-fight stance pick (bal / agg / grd / cun) is gone from the UI. The strategic layer is now a **plan chosen in the prep sheet** (`js/flow.js prep`, panel in `js/screens/plan.js`, styles in the tagged `PLAN` block of `css/ui.css`). In-fight orders (Press, Hold, Fall back, Guard, Rally, Swap, tap an enemy) are unchanged and are now the quick overrides; the wheel and hint say "the plan was set before the fight".

## Data (core/sim.js, section BATTLE PLAN) and the plan object
`plan = { v: 1, form, roles: { [gladId]: roleId }, prio: index into the foes | null, show: 0..3 }`. `LM.normPlan(plan, glads)` repairs any value (known form, a role for everyone, show only for a solo fighter). It travels through every old `stance` argument: `prep` returns `{stance: plan}`, `fightScreen` / `playFight` / `buildFight` accept a plan object **or** the old string (a string means no roles, no show, no AI plan, exactly the old behaviour: hordes, hunts, pit, champion and bounty still pass `'bal'`).
- **Roles** `LM.ROLES` (id, n, d, mods, dx, ky, gain, cost; gain / cost lines are generated from `mods`, so the printed numbers are always the real ones): Fighter (none), Vanguard (+10% HP, +8% armor, -6% damage, starts 1.4 m forward, foes' target weight x0.8), Flanker (+12% speed, +5% crit, -6% HP, -4% armor, starts wide, arcs round to the weak and ranged foe), Anchor (+15% armor, +7% HP, -7% damage, trails the team while a partner is free, covers allies being hit), Hunter (+8% damage, -8% armor, never drops the priority target unless someone is within 2.2 m and the mark is over 3.5 m away), Skirmisher (+8% dodge, -4% HP, -3% damage, starts back, steps back when pressed while its blow recovers).
- **Formations** `LM.FORMS` (`pos(i, n)` start spot for side A, mirrored for B; `roles(n)` default roles; `mods` a printed team-wide trade applied to every unit): Line (flat, all Fighter, +3% damage and armor), Wedge (tip Vanguard, wings Hunter and Flanker, -4% armor), Pincer (wide Flankers, +4% damage), Turtle (tight, Vanguard in the middle, Anchors around, +2% armor). A 1% stat is worth about 1.7 points of win rate in these fights, so small numbers matter. Starting spots are clamped to 97% of the arena radius.
- **Priority target** (`plan.prio`, `LM.PRIO`): `LM.planFight(F, pa, pb)` sets `F.foc[side] = 'B' + prio` (the same focus a tap sets) and `F.pri[side] = true`. Everyone goes for it (Vanguards and Anchors only when it is within reach). Price: the marked unit takes 6% less damage (`PRIO.guard`, applied in `strike` when `F.pri` of the attacker's side is set and the target is the focus). When the mark falls, `refocus` picks the weakest foe left (lowest hp, first on a tie) while `F.pri` is set; a plain tap focus in a fight without a plan never refocuses.
- **Showmanship** `LM.SHOW[0..3]` (Efficient, Showman, Crowd-pleaser, Flourish): gain = `fame` bonus (+0 / 12 / 28 / 50%), `hy` hype multiplier on the fighter's hits, `fan` fans (`LM.favGain(g, win, hype, show)`, half in defeat); cost = `atk` and `def` reduction applied to the unit at creation, `wd` longer wind-up (`LM.windOf(u)`, the view uses the same number for the attack clip speed), a pose every `gap` s that costs `cast` s (event `flourish`, fightview plays the existing `taunt` clip), and `wound` extra injury chance in `LM.settle`. `res.show` comes from `fightResult` (`F.meta.show`); tournaments average the rounds' levels. Only fights that settle through `LM.settle` offer the dial (pit and hunts do not).
- **AI**: `LM.oppPlan(S, opps, gl)` is deterministic: formation from the styles on the field, roles from trait first (Hothead and Grudge: Hunter, Iron Jaw: Vanguard, Cool Head: Skirmisher...) then style, a priority target for every team (the hardest hitter for a Hothead or Grudge leader, the softest otherwise, read from real stats), Showman trait = Showmanship 1 in a duel. Beasts get no plan. In team fights the AI keeps `'bal'` as its stat stance (the roles carry the trade-offs); a solo foe keeps `LM.oppStance`.
- **Suggested plan** `LM.defaultPlan(S, gl, opps)`: formation by `pickForm(styles)` (two tanks: Turtle, two fast styles: Pincer, a Murmillo: Wedge, else Line) with that formation's own roles, priority `LM.suggestFoc(opps)` (the softest by style and level, public information so no fog leak). One tap on "Fight!" uses it.
- **Old saves**: `entries[key].stance` may still be bal / agg / grd / cun; `LM.planFromStance` maps them (Aggressive to Wedge, Guarded to Turtle, Cunning to Pincer, Balanced to the suggestion, solo to Efficient), `LM.entryPlan(S, key, glads, opps)` opens the prep sheet on an entry's plan or its mapped stance. `LM.enter(S, ev, ids, plan)` stores the plan in `entries[key].plan` next to `stance: 'bal'`. `LM.sanitize` mends unknown stances and drops broken plans (no `SAVE_VER` bump). Save key unchanged.

## Sim rules kept
No `rnd()` anywhere in the plan code (a test counts the draws of `mkUnits` with and without a plan), unit fields `role` (`''` = none), `show`, `flT` are declared in `blankUnit` (82 keys), the fight declares `pri`, no status key was added. `LM.odds` accepts a plan and simulates both sides' plans like the real fight.

# Update: FATE, the decision at the end of a won fight (kill / spare / ransom)
Code: `core/sim.js` (block "FATE", right where `LM.mercy` used to be), UI in `js/ui_kit.js` (`fatePanel`), `js/fightview.js` (section "THE DECISION"), results in `js/flow.js` (`fateStep`, `fateDock`, `joinDialog`, `fateEl`).
- **Where**: `LM.FATE_KINDS` = duel, tourney, grand (every round you win), team (the last enemy down). Hunts, pit floors, horde waves, bounties, the champion duel and sparring keep their own endings. Add a kind there only if its flow calls `fateStep` (flow.js) or the fight view can ask it.
- **API** (pure, exact numbers): `LM.fateOffers(S, ev, foe)` returns `{opts:[kill, spare, ransom|show], def:'spare', owner}` with `fame gold hype fac temple ludus join vend gain[] cost[]`; the panel and the dock print `gain`/`cost` as they are, `LM.fate(S, ev, rep, choice, foe)` applies the same numbers (rep optional) and returns `{id, fame, gold, joined, head, gain, cost}`. No choice or an unknown one means spare. `LM.mercy` is a thin legacy wrapper.
- **Numbers** (b = base fame of ONE bout: the duel's `ev.fame`, else the league's `fameWin`; purse likewise): Kill `+round(0.4b)+4` fame, crowd hype +15, Plebs +4, Senate -5, Temple of Mars regard -8 (only with a temple contract), the rival school's regard -10 (when `foe.lud` names one of the four `S.ludi` schools), a VENDETTA on his `rv` for 12 weeks (his school's fighters hit +8%, `arena.js dmgMul`, set in `fightsetup.js`, shown in `prep`). Spare `+round(0.2b)+2` fame, hype +6, Senate +6, Plebs +2, Temple +3, school +3, recruit chance (`LM.fateJoin`: 30% base, +2% per Barracks level, +6% if he is not above your best, -6% if 3+ levels above, -8% proud traits hothead/grudge/bloodlust, +5% humble frugal/underdog/coolhead, +4% with a showman in the ludus, clamp 8..60%, 0 with a full barracks). Ransom `max(20, round((0.45 purse + 8 lvl) * wealth))` gold (wealth = the school's `str` 0.85..1.2, else 1), `+round(0.05b)+1` fame, Merchants +4, Plebs -1, school +6; only when `foe.lud` exists and he is not a ghost, else the third option is Showboat (`round(0.2 purse)+5` gold, Merchants +5, hype +4). Stats `kills`, `ransoms` (spares unchanged), trophies Blood Sport (5 kills) and Ransomer (3 ransoms).
- **Hype** is added to `res.hype` in `fightResult` (`LM.fateHype`), so it also feeds the settle fame multiplier a few percent.
- **Save/replay**: nothing new is saved except stats and `S.rivals[rv].vend` (week). The choice rides in `F.replay.fate = {choice, foe, n}` (result phase, never read by the sim); replays and the highlight reel play the recorded choice. Old saves: `LM.migrate` zero-fills the new stats.
- **Auto paths**: `F.autoA` (auto-fight) takes the default (spare) after a 6 s countdown shown on the button; skipped fights, ranged kills and fast-forward get the same options in the docked results sheet (`fateDock`); no choice at all (bots, tools that call `LM.settle` directly) means spare. `tools/ludus3d_bot.mjs --fate kill|spare|ransom|cycle` and `driveFlow` in `tools/ludus3d_qa_lib.mjs` click `.lx-fate-opt` (and audit the panel first).
- Pitfall: the fight view is the only place that knows the last foe (`ex.dst`); `fateFoe(res)` in flow.js falls back to the strongest enemy when the arena did not ask. A tournament pays the fate per round (S is changed at once, the totals are added to `rep` afterwards, `rep.fates` feeds the verdict section).
