# VENATIO: beasts, hunts, bestiary (Ludus Maximus 3D)

Files: `js/beast.js` (rigs + animator), `js/beast_fx.js` (fight view presentation), `js/screens/venatio.js` (UI + hunt flow), `core/ext.js` section 5 (data, hunts, rewards, bestiary), `core/arena.js` (beast units + AI, search for BEAST), `js/audio.js` (beast sfx block), `lab/beast_lab.html` (orbit / pose sheets / `?test=1`), tests in `tests/ludus_maximus_3d.test.mjs` (round 47) and the structure test (section 12).

## Sim (no counters, just tempo and numbers)
- `D.BEASTS[kind]`: `{n, role, c, ai, sz, h, range, ms, aps, mul, base, fur, fur2, mane, enrage:{at,atk,aps,ms}, boss, pelt, val, names, d, lore[4]}`. Beast "styles" are defined in `D.STYLES` with `Object.defineProperty(..., enumerable:false)`: every `D.STYLES[u.style].n/.c/.mul/.base/.beats` lookup (nameplates, splash, derive, adv) works, but `D.STYLE_IDS`, `Object.keys(D.STYLES)` and every recruit / style picker never list them. `beats` is `[]` so `LM.adv` is 0 against every style.
- `LM.makeBeast(kind, lvl, {r, id, power, pw})` returns a record shaped like a gladiator (`st`, `tal`, `style = beast = kind`, `look`) so `mkUnits`, `derive`, `odds`, `teamCtx`, portraits and fightResult take it unchanged. `LM.ultsOf` returns `[]` for beasts.
- `mkUnit` (arena.js) wraps the gladiator builder and adds `u.beast, u.bx, u.ai, u.range, u.ms, u.aps, u.sz, u.boss, u.bs` (AI timers). `g.pw` (hunt tuning) scales HP and sqrt(atk).
- AI (`beastThink`, `bAtk`, `beastHit`, `bCharge`, `bPounce`, `bRoar`, `bEnrage`): `pack` (wolves circle at 3.5 m on their own angle, rush in turns, bite and fade; every wolf near the target adds +10% bite damage up to 3), `pounce` (lion, Nemean: crouch 0.38 s then leap, bleeding bite, springs clear of a clinch to leap again about every 7 s; boss mauls every 4th blow and roars at 75%), `bear` (cleave swipe, mauling every 3rd blow, roar slow at 65%), `boar` (0.55 s paw telegraph, straight charge, knockup, winded), `trample` (elephant: stomp AoE with slow, trample charge 0.85 s telegraph, trunk swat cleave, handler javelins every ~4 s). Enrage flips once under `bx.enrage.at` (+aps, +ms through `apsOf`/`msOf`, +atk in `dmgMul`).
- Events emitted: ordinary `atk` (with `beast: 'bite'|'claw'|'pounce'|'maul'|'swipe'|'swat'|'gore'` and `w` = windup), `hit` (tag = attack kind or `charge`, `trample`, `stomp`, `javelin`), `aoe` (stomp), `proj` (javelin), `dash`, `status` (bleed, rage) and the new `{k:'beast', a:'roar'|'howl'|'enrage'|'telegraph'|'charge'|'winded'|'land'|'stomp'|'javelin'}`. `motion` honours `dash.sp` (own speed) and `dash.tick` (per step hook); `separate` uses `u.sz` for bigger bodies.
- Hunts: `D.HUNTS` (`kinds`, `party`, `lo` level offset, `min` league, `g`/`f` gold and fame multipliers of the league purse, `pw`, `risk`, `boss`). `LM.venatioState(S)` (open via Stands level 1, league >= 1, or a festival of Mars / Bacchus / Imperial Visit; one hunt per week via `S.venatio.week`), `LM.venatioHunts`, `LM.huntBeasts` (seeded by save seed and key, stable all week), `LM.venatioSettle(S, hunt, party, res)` (gold, fame, pelts, trophies, rare item, XP, injuries, bestiary), `LM.bestiary`, `LM.sellPelts`, `LM.trophyFame` (+weekly fame via `LM.extWeek`). Mortality kind `hunt` (7% base, +2% per risk level, +8% boss) uses the normal `deathRoll` / `fall` / `fallenFlow`.
- Save: everything lives in `S.venatio` (created on demand and in `LM.migrate`); no key was renamed.
- Balance method: `LM.quick` parties of equal level, ungeared, q 0.5 (three style sets) against each hunt, 40 fights. Targets: 55 to 80% for fair hunts, 45 to 65% for the bosses. Tune ONLY the per hunt `pw` (the curve is steep: +0.1 pw can swing 30 points). Typical measured: wolves 65%, boars 63%, lion 63 to 93%, bears 80%, pride 82%, menagerie 70%, Nemean 55%, elephant 55%.

## Rig (js/beast.js)
- `createBeast(unitOrRecord, {detail, scale, look})` returns a rig with the gladiator contract: `root, body, joints, animator, update(dt), flash, squashKick, localDir, setPalette, resetSecondary, dispose, addBlood/setBlood/addWound/clearBlood/jointRest, severed{}, sever()->null`, plus `beast, size, nameH, beastR, portraitD, triangles(), drawCalls(), seed`.
- Skeleton: `hips, spine, chest, neck, head, jaw, earL/R, tail1..3`, front legs `shoulderL/R, elbow, wrist` (paw), hind legs `hipL/R, knee, ankle` (paw), plus the life joints `eyeL/R` (the eyeball turns, the iris and pupil are vertex colours), `lidL/R` (a dome hinged over the eye: 0 open, 1.3 shut), `mane1/mane2` (neck and chest, the lion mane, wolf ruff and tail tufts sway on them), elephant `trunk1..4`, `rider`, `riderArm`, `flag1/flag2` (banner poles). Names reuse the humanoid ones on purpose: gore.js places wounds on `chest/spine/shoulder/elbow/hip/head`, jets on `chest`, footprints on `ankleL/R`. `jointRest` scales gore's humanoid offsets by the beast size.
- Conventions (same as the gladiator): faces +Z, +X left, limbs hang along -Y, NEGATIVE x swings a leg FORWARD, positive neck/head x pitches DOWN, positive jaw x opens the mouth, positive `bodyP` pitches the nose DOWN. Joints have no rest rotation; the rest pose is bent through the offsets (spec tables `LION`, `WOLF`, `BEAR`, `BOAR`, `ELEPHANT`: `front/hind {sy, up:[dy,dz], lo:[dy,dz], pad}`). `chestY` and `hipsY` are DERIVED so the paws stand on y = 0.
- Geometry kit (all in REST SPACE, body space, so skin weights are easy to reason about): `loft(ctrl, {seg, sub, cap, up, infl})` is a smooth surface through control rings (Catmull-Rom, rounded caps, parallel transport frames, auto winding) with a per vertex control parameter `rs`; `skinKnots(geo, [[bone, controlIndex], ...], bz)` turns it into REAL smooth skinning (up to 4 bones per vertex, blended over +-bz control spans around each joint). The torso (hips, spine, chest), neck (chest, neck, head), every leg (scapula or hips, hip/shoulder, knee/elbow, hock/wrist, paw), the tail and the trunk are one continuous skin each. `Blades` batches thin tapered strand cards (mane layers, ruff, tufts, whiskers, bristle crest, plumes) with per vertex weights; `addSkin2` rigs a piece to two bones; `setColor(geo, fn)` writes LINEAR vertex colours (underbelly gradient, socks, muzzle, ear inner, mane root to tip) because the fur material is white and multiplies them. Never use `new Color(true)`: two keys with the same name in a spec silently overrode `mane` colour with a boolean once (flag is `hasMane`).
- Anatomy per species: lion (broad chest, deep ribcage, muscular haunch, brow ridge, cheek and whisker pads, nose pad, lip line, canines, tufted tail, elbow tufts, a dark core plus 230 layered strand cards on the mane joints), wolf (narrow deep chest, long legs with a dark foreleg stripe, bushy tail, pale ruff cards, pointed ears), bear (hump, heavy forelimbs, small round ears, long snout, shaggy fringe cards), boar (wedge head, nose disc, tusks, dorsal bristle crest, hooves), Nemean (lion with a black mane, gold glints and plates, scars), war elephant (domed skull, segmented trunk skin, wrinkled flapping ears, curved tusks, blanket with gold trim, howdah with rails and corner finials, a shield, two tall banners that stream on the flag joints, an armoured handler: banded cuirass, pteruges, crested helmet, bracers, javelin).
- Materials: ONE skinned mesh per material. Animals: `fur` (body), `shell` (high tier only, three fur shell layers re-lofted a little larger and cut into strand dots by a noise discard in the shader), `mane` (DoubleSide cards), `bone` (claws, teeth, tusks), `eye` (nose, mouth, eyes) = 4 to 5 calls; elephant adds `leather, cloth, skin, metal, flag` = 8 (the limit). The fur shader is `shadePatch('cloth'|'leather')` plus: strand grain noise, derivative bump (fur relief, elephant wrinkles), patchy colour variation, a warm velvet rim, a dark contour on grazing edges (silhouette against the sand), a warm sand bounce on the undersides, and its OWN blood model. Vertex position (`vBP`) is the rest-space position, so noise sticks to the body. `FURK` holds the per species numbers.
- Blood on fur (rig.js `shadePatch` soak is switched off with a string replace): a wound keeps its own `soak` in `uWoundD.w`; `addBlood()` puts the blood into the wound that gore.js just placed (soak +2.4x, the others +0.12x), the whole coat only gets a small spatter low on the body (`uBlood` is scaled by 0.55 and capped). The shader darkens the fur (multiplies it, so the strand grain stays visible), with wet clumps made from stretched noise, a runnel that falls with gravity, a darker core and a thin gash, fresh blood redder and wet (low roughness), dried blood brown. Gore.js is untouched.
- Geometry cache: the merged skinned geometry of a (species, tier, coat, mane) is built once (`GEO_CACHE`, 9 entries, LRU) and shared by every rig of it (`geometry.userData.shared`, never disposed by `rig.dispose()`); a second wolf builds in about 15 ms instead of 100 to 1000 ms. `rig._items` is empty on a cache hit.
- Tiers (`DETAIL`): low (seg 4, no sub samples, no shells, 22 mane cards), medium (seg 7, 2 samples, cards 70, lids and teeth), high (seg 10, cards 230, 3 shell layers, whiskers, claws). Measured tris low / medium / high: lion 860 / 3798 / 10249, wolf 722 / 3057 / 7200, bear 724 / 3012 / 7024, boar 840 / 3199 / 7242, Nemean 988 / 3926 / 10377, elephant 1546 / 4740 / 6700. Calls: animals 3 / 4 / 5, elephant 8 on every tier. Check with `node` (see Recipes) or `lab/beast_lab.html?test=1`.
- Animator: base layer = idle life + gait, action layer = sparse keyed clips (only the channels a clip names override the base), additive hurt spring, snapshot crossfade on every state or clip change, ground fix. A channel first keyed late eases in from 0 (it used to hold its first value from t = 0). Gait: walk, trot or gallop by speed (elephants pace), cycle advanced by DISTANCE travelled, leg swing sized from stride x duty so a stance paw travels as far as the body (`half = stride * duty / 2`, no skating), swing paws lift and fold, body bob, spine wave in the gallop, head nod. PAW PLANTING (`plant()`): after the pose is written and the matrices updated, each paw that should be on the ground (idle, or in stance) is put exactly on the sand by one analytic elbow/knee step (`delta = -err / (axis x r).y`), a paw below the sand is lifted whatever it does, paws a clip moves (a raised paw in a claw) are left alone (`legW`), airborne clips skip it. Idle life (`life()`): breathing, a head wander with eyes that lead and counter the head, blinks every 3 to 6 s (lids heavier at rest, narrowed when fighting), a fidget every ~3 s chosen by hash of the time slot (look around, ear flick, tail swish, weight shift, paw lift, then per species: lion yawn, wolf sniffs, bear sways its head, boar roots, elephant trunk lift with ears out), secondary springs for tail, ears, mane and banners driven by turn rate, speed change and bob. Steps emit `rig.onEvent('step', {side, front})` at touchdown for gore footprints and paw thuds.
- Clips (`clipsFor`): bite (coil with the jaw wide, lunge with the whole body, snap, head shake), claw (weight back, paw high, strike across with a body twist), swipe (= swat), maul (bite, double rake, bite), gore (head back, down, hook up), pounce (flat crouch, leap, crash, absorb, 1.5 s), roar (bears and elephants rear on the hind legs, mane flares, the animator adds the jaw tremble), howl, paw (charge telegraph, loops until `release()`), stomp, javelin (rider arm), hurt (additive, one eye screwed shut, tail whip), stun (loop), knockdown / getup, death (staggering step, forelegs fold, hindquarters sag, topple with a bounce, one last shudder, eyes closed, held), victory, and the gladiator aliases `attack1..3, block, taunt, cheer`. Pose shorthands in `clip()`: `ears, lids, mane, sh, hp, el, kn` set both sides. `clipInfo(name)` gives `{dur, swingAt, hitAt, recoverAt}`; `beast_fx.atk` sets the speed so the clip hit frame meets the sim windup.
- No dismemberment: `sever()` returns null, gore.js `popArmor` returns for `rig.beast`.

## Fight view (js/beast_fx.js, hooks tagged BEAST in fightview.js)
`makeRig` (also `prefetchBusts`: queues the VS splash portraits of both sides at the sizes bracket.js asks for, so they are cached when the splash opens), `setup` (paw thuds and a little dust for beasts that FEEL big: `bigOf(rig)` = max(rig size, nameH / 2.2), so the war elephant counts although its rig size is 1; camera nudge for >= 2), `atk`, `hitFx` (bite / claw / gore / slam sounds, dust and trauma for heavy blows, beast hurt voices), `down` (death cry, thud, boss banner and crowd roar), `event` (roar, howl, enrage banner + red ring + crowd wave, charge telegraph lane, land, stomp, javelin). Extra edits: `MASS` table (heavier beasts shrug off shoves), director `units[].h/.r` (framing for tall and wide beasts), nameplate height `rig.nameH`, `main.js ?demo=1&hunt=lion|wolves|boars|bears|pride|menagerie|nemean|elephant[&team=n]` (`demoHunt`). canExec (executions) returns false for beasts because they have no finisher clips.
Audio (additive block in audio.js, all synthesized): `beastRoar` (kind: lion, bear, boar squeal, Nemean sub-roar, elephant trumpet), `beastHowl`, `beastGrowl`, `beastHurt`, `beastDeath`, `beastBite`, `beastClaw`, `beastGore`, `beastSlam`, `pawThud`, `beastSnort`, `beastCharge`. Pass `{kind}` and `{pitch}`.

## VS splash and portraits (round 4)
The splash lives in js/bracket.js (`vsIntro`, `bustOf`). It used to wipe a cached portrait (removing and re-setting the canvas width) and show empty navy arches until the first render finished (a first render includes the shader compile: 4 to 40 s on software GL). Now: busts are prefetched when the fight starts (beast_fx `prefetchBusts`), a slot that is not ready shows a placeholder painted on the canvas (the beast's emblem medallion, or a head and shoulders silhouette for a gladiator) and the real render draws over it, and beast portraits use the medium rig (strand cards and lids, no fur shells). Framing: portrait.js `beastFrame` + `BUST_AIM`, camera distance `rig.portraitD`. Check with `lab/beast_lab.html?view=portrait`.

## UI
- Arena > Challenges: `venatioCard` (hunt rows with party size, level, gold, fame, risk) opens `startHunt`: hunt -> `pickFighters` (death risk and win odds badges, party synergy footer) -> `prep` (battle plan only, `pit:true` hides bets and tricks) -> 3D fight -> report (gold, fame, pelts, XP, injuries, loot) -> trophy dialogs -> `mortalCheck({kind:'hunt'})` -> `awardTrophies`. The arena reopens on Challenges.
- World > Bestiary (`bestiaryTab`): 3D portrait of every beast (`ctx.portrait(record)` -> portrait.js `beastFrame`; unmet beasts are silhouettes), kills, first sighting, role, description, stats after 1 kill, lore notes at 0 / 1 / 3 / 6 kills, pelt sale, trophy slots. Memorial and flow text know the kind `hunt` ("the Venatio").

## Recipes
- Quick budget check without a browser: `node` with `globalThis.window = globalThis`, require core/data.js, sim.js, ext.js, `import('./js/beast.js')`, `createBeast(LM.makeBeast(kind, 6, {r: LM.mulberry(5), id: 't'}), {detail})`, then `rig.triangles()`, `rig.drawCalls()`, and per item `rig._items` (mat, geo, skin).
- New beast: add a spec table in beast.js (copy WOLF, set `front/hind` chains, torso radii, head, colours, gait), a `D.BEASTS` entry (stats, lore, trophy in `D.BEAST_TROPHIES`), pick an `ai` (or write a new branch in `beastThink`), add MASS and VOICE_PITCH in beast_fx.js, a `FURK` entry, check `lab/beast_lab.html?beast=<id>` in grid / sheet (`view=sheet&anim=run&times=0,.1,.2,.3`), run `?test=1`, then balance the hunt `pw`.
- New hunt: add to `D.HUNTS`, tune `pw` with `LM.quick` loops, run the round 47 tests.

## Weaknesses (honest list)
Faces are plush rather than sculpted (the muzzle is one loft, no nostril or lip geometry beyond a lip line), the mane cards read as paper strips from some angles, fur shells are high tier only, the foot plant fixes height not sliding (the stride/duty sizing keeps sliding small below a trot, a sprint still slips), the elephant legs are columns with a knee bump, the handler has no face, beasts only use one pose per attack (no directional variants), no allied beast AI tuning, the first portrait of a session still waits for a shader compile (the VS splash now prefetches the busts and shows a medallion until they land), the near top-down phone camera (director) hides most of every beast.

## Round 3 note
Beasts ignore player orders (`LM.command` refuses beast units) and foe tactics (`aiCmd` returns for `u.beast`); their telegraph lanes stay in beast_fx.js. The tactics overlay draws team rings around beasts scaled by `rig.beastR`.

## Wave 3: bestiary art and Venatio trophies
- `js/glyphs.js` `emblem(kind, {size, color, boss, locked})` returns an SVG medallion (radial colour, dotted ring, cream line head from the glyph table, rays for bosses, a "?" silhouette when locked). The Bestiary tab shows portrait plus emblem, an italic lore quote, stats, six kill dots (squares mark the notes at 1, 3 and 6 kills), trophy pills, an empty state with six locked emblems and a hint, and for unmet beasts the hunt where they appear.
- Nine Venatio trophies are in `D.TROPHIES` (kill each beast, solo lion, menagerie, all pelts); they use `V.kills`, `V.solo` and `V.cleared` and pay gold, fame and laurels once. The older `D.BEAST_TROPHIES` (pelt drops that pay weekly fame) are unchanged.
