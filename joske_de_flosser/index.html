Punch chain — jab, cross, hook. The third one puts them down.
Kick chain — shin, body, head. The head kick launches them.
Mix them — the chain is shared: punch, punch, kick ends on the head kick.
Special — spend a stock for the Mega Floss: nine strikes, nothing can touch you, and the last one clears the room. Fill the bar by fighting, or pick up a gold token. You start with three.
Jump kick — jump, then kick. The bread and butter.
Whirl — punch + kick together. Out-flosses everyone around you, costs a little health.
Elbow — punch with somebody standing behind you.
Grab — walk into a dazed enemy. Then punch to knee, kick to throw.
Dash — tap left or right twice, then punch for a shoulder charge.
Weapons — stand over a bat, pipe, knife or crate and press punch.
