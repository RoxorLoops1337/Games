HOW TO PLAY

●

Hold on the machine to shoot coins, slide to aim. The pusher shoves coins over the edge. Coins that fall in the middle come back to you; the red corners eat them.

×3

Push tokens off the edge to fill the row of 6. Three of a colour and that hero attacks: blue Rox, pink Jas, yellow Mew. Attacks level heroes up.

◎

Beat the enemy to spin the Galaxy Wheel. The ball builds a coin tower (blue 30, yellow 36, pink 60, JP 80). Shove it till it topples!

x2

Every coin you win charges Energy. Full energy starts Double Time: double damage and double coins.
