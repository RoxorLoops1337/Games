HOW TO PLAY

●

Hold the left or right side of the machine (or the FIRE buttons) to shoot coins from that corner. Coins pushed over the edge score points; the red corners eat them. Coins to shoot refill over time and when you beat an enemy.

×3

Tokens shoot out of the port in the back wall. Push them off the edge to fill the row of 6 (2 seconds later they fly back out of the port). Three of a colour: that hero attacks and a shooter fires free coins (blue 10, yellow 15, pink 25).

◎

Beat the enemy to spin the Galaxy Wheel. The ball builds a coin tower (300, 400, 600, 900 or the 1200 jackpot). It rides the coins slowly to the edge and smacks against the glass!

x2

Every three of a kind grows your combo (more points per coin) and fills the meter; a full meter starts Double Time. Fill all 6 slots without a three and the combo breaks.
